## Mixed review: signals, power, digital and more

This second review covers the rest of the FE Electrical and Computer specification. Each section recalls the key facts in the form the handbook uses, with a worked problem. Treat it as a checklist: anything that feels unfamiliar is worth revisiting in its course.

## Signals and systems

- Laplace transform of a first-order system $H(s) = \dfrac{K}{\tau s + 1}$: DC gain $K$, time constant $\tau$.
- Fourier series of a square wave of amplitude $A$: odd harmonics only, fundamental amplitude $4A/\pi$.
- Sampling: $f_s > 2f_{max}$, or aliases appear at $|f - kf_s|$.

> $H(s) = \dfrac{10}{s + 5} = \dfrac{2}{0.2s + 1}$: DC gain 2, time constant 0.2 s, settling (2%) in about $4\tau = 0.8$ s.

## Power

- Three-phase power: $P = \sqrt3V_{LL}I_L\cos\theta$.
- Transformers: $V_1/V_2 = N_1/N_2 = I_2/I_1$; rated current $= S/V$.
- Induction motors: $n_s = 120f/p$, slip $s = (n_s - n)/n_s$.
- Per-unit: $Z_{base} = V_{base}^2/S_{base}$.

> A 480 V, three-phase load draws 50 A at 0.9 pf: $P = \sqrt3(480)(50)(0.9) = 37.4$ kW.
>
> A 50 kVA, 2400/240 V transformer's secondary rated current: $50\,000/240 = 208$ A.
>
> A 4-pole, 60 Hz motor at 1750 rpm: $n_s = 1800$, slip $= 2.8\%$.

## Electromagnetics

- Coulomb's law $F = kq_1q_2/r^2$; capacitance $C = \varepsilon A/d$; inductance of a solenoid $L = \mu N^2A/\ell$.
- Transmission lines: $\Gamma = (Z_L - Z_0)/(Z_L + Z_0)$, VSWR $= (1 + |\Gamma|)/(1 - |\Gamma|)$.

## Control systems

- Second-order: $\omega_n$, $\zeta$; %OS depends only on $\zeta$; $t_s \approx 4/(\zeta\omega_n)$.
- Steady-state error from the system type; stability from pole locations or Routh–Hurwitz; margins from Bode plots.

## Communications

- AM bandwidth $2f_m$; FM (Carson) $2(\Delta f + f_m)$.
- Shannon capacity $C = B\log_2(1 + S/N)$.

> A 3 kHz channel at 30 dB SNR: $C = 3000\log_2(1001) = 29.9$ kb/s.

## Digital systems and computers

- Number systems: binary, hexadecimal, two's complement ($-x$ = invert all bits and add 1).
- Boolean algebra and K-maps: $AB + A\bar B = A$; De Morgan: $\overline{AB} = \bar A + \bar B$.
- Flip-flops, counters, state machines; timing: $T_c \ge t_{pcq} + t_{pd} + t_{setup}$.
- Memory: $2^n$ addresses for $n$ address bits; cache AMAT $= t_{hit} + \text{miss rate} \times \text{penalty}$.

> 0x3F $= 3 \times 16 + 15 = 63$. In 8-bit two's complement, $-20$ is $256 - 20 = 236$ = 0xEC.

## Software and engineering practice

- Algorithm complexity (big-O), basic data structures, and reading short code fragments.
- Ethics (Lesson 2.1), safety (Lesson 2.2), engineering economics (present worth, interest), and intellectual property.

> **Engineering economics.** The present worth of \$1000 received in 5 years at 6% interest is $1000/1.06^5 = 747$ dollars. The handbook's interest tables give the same factor, $(P/F, 6\%, 5) = 0.7473$.

## Practice (timed: aim for 3 minutes each)

```quiz
? What is the time constant of $H(s) = 4/(s + 20)$, in ms?
= 50 | 0.001 | ms
> $\tau = 1/20$ s.
```

```quiz
? A square wave of amplitude 5 V has what fundamental Fourier amplitude, in V?
= 6.366 | 0.002 | V
> $4(5)/\pi$.
```

```quiz
? A 208 V three-phase load draws 30 A at 0.85 pf. What is its real power, in kW?
= 9.187 | 0.005 | kW
> $\sqrt3(208)(30)(0.85)$.
```

```quiz
? A 6-pole, 50 Hz induction motor runs at 960 rpm. What is its slip, in %?
= 4 | 0.001 | %
> $(1000 - 960)/1000$.
```

```quiz
? What is the decimal value of hexadecimal 0xA7?
= 167 | 0.001 |
> $10 \times 16 + 7$.
```

```quiz
? How is $-1$ represented in 8-bit two's complement, as an unsigned value?
= 255 | 0.001 |
> 0xFF.
```

```quiz
? What is the present worth of 5000 dollars received in 10 years at 5% interest, in dollars?
= 3069.6 | 0.002 |
> $5000/1.05^{10}$.
```

```quiz
? A load of 75 Ω terminates a 50 Ω line. What is the VSWR?
= 1.5 | 0.001 |
> $\Gamma = 0.2$.
```

```quiz
? The Boolean expression $AB + A\bar B$ simplifies to:
+ $A$
- $B$
- $AB$
- $1$
> $A(B + \bar B) = A$.
```
