Covers Lessons 4.1–4.2: output stages and oscillators.

```quiz
? A class B stage delivers a 10 V peak sine into 8 Ω. What is the load power, in W?
= 6.25 | 0.001 | W
> $V_p^2/(2R_L) = 100/16$.
```

```quiz
? With $\pm12$ V supplies, what is that stage's efficiency, in %?
= 65.45 | 0.005 | %
> $\frac{\pi}{4}\cdot\frac{10}{12} = 65.4\%$.
```

```quiz
? What is the worst-case total transistor dissipation of a class B stage with $\pm12$ V supplies and an 8 Ω load, in W?
= 3.648 | 0.005 | W
> $2V_{CC}^2/(\pi^2R_L) = 288/78.96 = 3.65$ W.
```

```quiz
? A Wien-bridge oscillator uses $R = 1$ kΩ and $C = 100$ nF. What is its frequency, in Hz?
= 1591.5 | 0.005 | Hz
> $1/(2\pi RC) = 1.59$ kHz.
```

```quiz
? A Colpitts oscillator has $L = 2\ \mu$H and $C_1 = C_2 = 1$ nF. What is its frequency, in MHz?
= 5.033 | 0.005 | MHz
> $C_{eq} = 0.5$ nF; $1/(2\pi\sqrt{2\times10^{-6} \cdot 0.5\times10^{-9}}) = 5.03$ MHz.
```

```quiz
? A three-stage ring oscillator has 100 ps per stage. What is its frequency, in GHz?
= 1.667 | 0.005 | GHz
> $1/(2 \cdot 3 \cdot 100\ \text{ps})$.
```
