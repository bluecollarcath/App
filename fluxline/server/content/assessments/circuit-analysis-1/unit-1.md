Covers Lessons 1.1–1.4: charge, current, voltage and power; Ohm's and Kirchhoff's laws; series, parallel and dividers; dependent sources and wye–delta. Numeric answers are accepted within about 1%.

```quiz
? A 9 V battery delivers a steady 0.5 A for 2 hours. How much energy does it deliver, in kJ?
= 32.4 | 0.01 | kJ
> $w = vit = 9 \times 0.5 \times 7200\ \text{s} = 32\,400$ J.
```

```quiz
? An element has 5 V across it ($+$ at the top), and a 2 A reference current *leaves* the top ($+$) terminal. What power does the element absorb, in W?
= -10 | 0.01 | W
> The current enters the $-$ terminal, so $p = -vi = -10$ W. The element delivers 10 W.
```

```quiz
? A 12 V source drives 1 kΩ, 2 kΩ and 3 kΩ resistors in series. What is the voltage across the 3 kΩ resistor, in V?
= 6 | 0.01 | V
> Voltage divider: $12 \cdot \frac{3}{1 + 2 + 3} = 6$ V.
```

```quiz
? What is the equivalent resistance of 20 Ω, 30 Ω and 60 Ω in parallel, in Ω?
= 10 | 0.01 | Ω
> $\frac{1}{R} = \frac{1}{20} + \frac{1}{30} + \frac{1}{60} = \frac{6}{60}$, so $R = 10\ \Omega$.
```

```quiz
? A 12 mA current divides between a 1 kΩ resistor and a 3 kΩ resistor in parallel. How much flows in the 3 kΩ resistor, in mA?
= 3 | 0.01 | mA
> $12 \cdot \frac{1}{1 + 3} = 3$ mA. The larger resistor gets the smaller share.
```

```quiz
? Currents of 4 A and 1.5 A enter a node. A current of 2.5 A leaves it through one branch. What current leaves through the only other branch, in A?
= 3 | 0.01 | A
> KCL: $4 + 1.5 - 2.5 - i = 0 \Rightarrow i = 3$ A.
```

```quiz
? A Δ network has $R_{ab} = 6\ \Omega$, $R_{bc} = 3\ \Omega$ and $R_{ca} = 9\ \Omega$. What is the resistance $R_a$ of the equivalent Y, in Ω?
= 3 | 0.01 | Ω
> $R_a = \frac{R_{ab}R_{ca}}{R_{ab} + R_{bc} + R_{ca}} = \frac{6 \cdot 9}{18} = 3\ \Omega$.
```
