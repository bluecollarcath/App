Covers Lessons 2.1–2.3: homogeneous second-order equations, forced response, and resonance in the series RLC.

```quiz

= -3 | 0.001 |
> $(s + 3)(s + 5)$.
```

```quiz
? $y'' + 4y' + 13y = 0$ oscillates at what frequency, in rad/s?
= 3 | 0.001 | rad/s
> $s = -2 \pm j3$.
```

```quiz
? What is the steady-state solution of $y'' + 5y' + 4y = 12$?
= 3 | 0.001 |
> $4A = 12$.
```

```quiz
? A series RLC has $L = 4$ mH and $C = 0.1$ µF. What is its resonant frequency, in kHz?
= 7.958 | 0.002 | kHz
> $\omega_0 = 1/\sqrt{4\times10^{-10}} = 5\times10^4$ rad/s.
```

```quiz
? With $R = 40\ \Omega$, what is its $Q$?
= 5 | 0.001 |
> $(1/40)\sqrt{0.004/10^{-7}} = 200/40$.
```

```quiz
? What resistance makes a series RLC with $L = 0.5$ H and $C = 2$ µF critically damped, in Ω?
= 1000 | 0.001 | Ω
> $2\sqrt{L/C} = 2\sqrt{250\,000}$.
```
