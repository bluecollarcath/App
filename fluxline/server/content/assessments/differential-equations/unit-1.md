Covers Lessons 1.1–1.3: first-order linear equations, separable and exact equations, and existence, uniqueness and equilibria.

```quiz
? Solve $y' + 4y = 8$ with $y(0) = 0$. What is $y(0.5)$?
= 1.7293 | 0.002 |
> $y = 2(1 - e^{-4t})$; $2(1 - e^{-2})$.
```

```quiz
? An RC circuit has $R = 20$ kΩ and $C = 50$ µF. What is its time constant, in s?
= 1 | 0.001 | s
> $RC$.
```

```quiz
? Solve $y' = 3t^2y$ with $y(0) = 2$. What is $y(1)$?
= 5.4366 | 0.002 |
> $y = 2e^{t^3}$; $2e$.
```

```quiz
? A part cools from 80 °C toward 30 °C with $k = 0.2$ min⁻¹. What is its temperature after 5 minutes, in °C?
= 48.39 | 0.002 | °C
> $30 + 50e^{-1}$.
```

```quiz
? What is the stable equilibrium of $y' = 6 - 2y$?
= 3 | 0.001 |
> $f(y) = 0$ at $y = 3$, with $f' = -2 < 0$.
```

```quiz
? The equation $y' = y^{1/3}$ with $y(0) = 0$:
+ has more than one solution, because $\partial f/\partial y$ is not continuous at $y = 0$
- has exactly one solution
- has no solution
- is linear
> Both $y = 0$ and $y = (2t/3)^{3/2}$ work.
```
