The final exam covers all three units of Semiconductor Devices. Use $n_i = 10^{10}$ cm⁻³, $kT/q = 0.0259$ V, $q = 1.6\times10^{-19}$ C, $\varepsilon_s = 1.036\times10^{-12}$ F/cm, $\varepsilon_{ox} = 3.45\times10^{-13}$ F/cm. Numeric answers are accepted within about 1%.

```quiz
? GaAs has a 1.42 eV bandgap. What wavelength does band-edge emission correspond to, in nm?
= 873.2 | 0.005 | nm
> $1240/1.42$.
```

```quiz
? Silicon has $N_A = 10^{16}$ cm⁻³. What is the electron concentration, in cm⁻³?
= 1e4 | 0.001 | cm⁻³
> $10^{20}/10^{16}$.
```

```quiz
? What is the conductivity of n-type silicon with $N_D = 10^{17}$ cm⁻³ and $\mu_n = 800$ cm²/V·s, in S/cm?
= 12.8 | 0.001 | S/cm
> $qN_D\mu_n$.
```

```quiz
? Holes with $\mu_p = 400$ cm²/V·s have what diffusion coefficient, in cm²/s?
= 10.36 | 0.005 | cm²/s
> $400 \times 0.0259$.
```

```quiz
? Holes with $D_p = 10$ cm²/s have a lifetime of 0.1 µs. What is their diffusion length, in µm?
= 10 | 0.001 | µm
> $\sqrt{10 \times 10^{-7}} = 10^{-3}$ cm.
```

```quiz
? What is $V_{bi}$ for $N_A = 10^{17}$ and $N_D = 10^{17}$ cm⁻³, in V?
= 0.8349 | 0.005 | V
> $0.0259\ln(10^{34}/10^{20})$.
```

```quiz
? A $p^+$–$n$ junction with $N_D = 10^{16}$ cm⁻³ and $V_{bi} = 0.8$ V is reverse biased at 10 V. What is its depletion width, in µm?
= 1.183 | 0.005 | µm
> $\sqrt{2\varepsilon_s(10.8)/(qN_D)} = 1.18\ \mu$m.
```

```quiz
? An ideal diode carries 1 mA at 0.6 V. What current does it carry at 0.66 V, in mA? (300 K)
= 10.13 | 0.01 | mA
> $e^{0.06/0.0259} = 10.1$.
```

```quiz
? A diode carries 5 mA with lifetime 0.5 µs. What is its diffusion capacitance, in nF?
= 96.53 | 0.005 | nF
> $(I/V_T)\tau = (0.005/0.0259)(5\times10^{-7})$.
```

```quiz
? A BJT has $\alpha = 0.995$. What is $\beta$?
= 199 | 0.001 |
> $0.995/0.005$.
```

```quiz
? What is $C_{ox}$ for a 10 nm oxide, in nF/cm²?
= 345 | 0.002 | nF/cm²
> $3.45\times10^{-13}/10^{-6} = 3.45\times10^{-7}$ F/cm².
```

```quiz
? $V_{FB} = -1$ V, $2\phi_F = 0.9$ V and the depletion term is 0.5 V. What is $V_T$, in V?
= 0.4 | 0.001 | V
> Sum the three terms.
```

```quiz
? $\mu_nC_{ox} = 250\ \mu$A/V², $W/L = 20$, $V_{GS} - V_T = 0.2$ V. What is the saturation current, in µA?
= 100 | 0.001 | µA
> $\tfrac12(250)(20)(0.04)$.
```

```quiz
? A MOSFET has $S = 90$ mV/dec. How many decades below its threshold current is it at $V_{GS} = V_T - 0.36$ V?
= 4 | 0.001 |
> $360/90$.
```

```quiz
? Which device is made fastest by the absence of stored minority charge?
+ the Schottky diode
- the p–n diode
- the Zener diode
- the bipolar transistor in saturation
> Schottky diodes conduct with majority carriers only.
```
