Covers Lessons 1.1–1.4: energy bands, carrier statistics and doping, transport, and recombination. Use $n_i = 10^{10}$ cm⁻³, $kT = 0.0259$ eV, $q = 1.6\times10^{-19}$ C.

```quiz
? Silicon's bandgap is 1.12 eV. What is the longest wavelength it can absorb, in µm?
= 1.107 | 0.005 | µm
> $\lambda = 1.24/E_g$.
```

```quiz
? Silicon is doped with $N_D = 10^{15}$ cm⁻³. What is the hole concentration, in cm⁻³?
= 1e5 | 0.001 | cm⁻³
> $n_i^2/N_D = 10^{20}/10^{15}$.
```

```quiz
? For $N_A = 10^{17}$ cm⁻³, how far below $E_i$ is the Fermi level, in eV?
= 0.4175 | 0.005 | eV
> $kT\ln(N_A/n_i) = 0.0259\ln(10^7)$.
```

```quiz
? What is the resistivity of p-type silicon with $N_A = 10^{18}$ cm⁻³ and $\mu_p = 250$ cm²/V·s, in Ω·cm?
= 0.025 | 0.005 | Ω·cm
> $1/(qN_A\mu_p) = 1/(1.6\times10^{-19} \times 10^{18} \times 250)$.
```

```quiz
? Electrons have $\mu_n = 1400$ cm²/V·s. What is $D_n$ at 300 K, in cm²/s?
= 36.26 | 0.005 | cm²/s
> $\mu kT/q$.
```

```quiz
? Minority carriers have $D = 10$ cm²/s and $\tau = 10\ \mu$s. What is their diffusion length, in µm?
= 100 | 0.001 | µm
> $\sqrt{10 \times 10^{-5}} = 0.01$ cm.
```

```quiz
? Excess carriers decay with lifetime $\tau$. What fraction remains after $\tau/2$?
= 0.6065 | 0.002 |
> $e^{-0.5}$.
```
