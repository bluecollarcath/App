Covers Lessons 1.1–1.4: charge and Coulomb's law, the electric field, Gauss's law, and electric potential. Use $k = 8.99\times10^9$ N·m²/C² and $\varepsilon_0 = 8.854\times10^{-12}$ F/m.

```quiz
? Charges of 3 µC and 2 µC are 10 cm apart. What force acts between them, in N?
= 5.394 | 0.002 | N
> $k(3\times10^{-6})(2\times10^{-6})/0.01$.
```

```quiz
? What is the field 3 cm from a 2 nC point charge, in kV/m?
= 19.98 | 0.002 | kV/m
> $k(2\times10^{-9})/0.0009$.
```

```quiz
? A closed surface encloses 5 nC. What is the net flux through it, in N·m²/C?
= 564.7 | 0.002 | N·m²/C
> $Q/\varepsilon_0$.
```

```quiz
? What is the potential 30 cm from a 6 nC charge (zero at infinity), in V?
= 179.8 | 0.002 | V
> $kQ/r$.
```

```quiz
? A large sheet carries 2 µC/m². What field does it produce, in kV/m?
= 112.9 | 0.002 | kV/m
> $\sigma/2\varepsilon_0$.
```

```quiz
? 50 V is applied across plates 2 mm apart. What is the field between them, in V/m?
= 25000 | 0.001 | V/m
> $V/d$.
```

```quiz
? Inside a conductor in electrostatic equilibrium:
+ the electric field is zero and any excess charge sits on the surface
- the field is uniform and nonzero
- charge is spread evenly through the volume
- the potential varies from point to point
> Free charges move until there's no force on them.
```
