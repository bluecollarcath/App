The final exam covers all three units of Physics: Electricity & Magnetism. Use $k = 8.99\times10^9$ N·m²/C², $\varepsilon_0 = 8.854\times10^{-12}$ F/m, $\mu_0 = 4\pi\times10^{-7}$ T·m/A, $e = 1.602\times10^{-19}$ C. Numeric answers are accepted within about 1%.

```quiz
? How many electrons make up −8 µC of charge? (Answer in units of $10^{13}$.)
= 4.994 | 0.002 |
> $8\times10^{-6}/1.602\times10^{-19} = 4.99\times10^{13}$.
```

```quiz
? Two 1 µC charges are 1 m apart. What force acts between them, in mN?
= 8.99 | 0.002 | mN
> $k \times 10^{-12}$.
```

```quiz
? A line charge of 4 nC/m produces what field at 20 cm, in V/m?
= 359.6 | 0.002 | V/m
> $2k\lambda/r$.
```

```quiz
? An electron is accelerated from rest through 1000 V. How much kinetic energy does it gain, in eV?
= 1000 | 0.001 | eV
> $e\Delta V$.
```

```quiz
? $V(x) = 50 - 4x^2$ volts. What is $E_x$ at $x = 1$ m, in V/m?
= 8 | 0.001 | V/m
> $-dV/dx = 8x$.
```

```quiz
? Capacitors of 2 µF and 3 µF are in parallel at 10 V. What total energy do they store, in µJ?
= 250 | 0.001 | µJ
> $\tfrac12(5\ \mu\text{F})(100)$.
```

```quiz
? What is the drift velocity in a 1 mm² copper wire ($n = 8.5\times10^{28}$ m⁻³) carrying 3 A, in mm/s?
= 0.2203 | 0.005 | mm/s
> $3/(8.5\times10^{28} \times 1.602\times10^{-19} \times 10^{-6})$.
```

```quiz
? A 1.5 V cell with 0.3 Ω internal resistance drives a 2.7 Ω load. What current flows, in A?
= 0.5 | 0.001 | A
> $1.5/3$.
```

```quiz
? A 0.2 m wire carrying 10 A sits perpendicular to a 0.5 T field. What force does it feel, in N?

```

```quiz
? What is the field at the center of a 50-turn coil of radius 5 cm carrying 1 A, in mT?

```

```quiz
? Two parallel wires 1 cm apart each carry 5 A. What is the force per meter between them, in mN/m?

```

```quiz
? A 20-turn coil of 0.01 m² sees a field change of 0.6 T in 30 ms. What emf is induced, in V?

```


