Covers Lessons 2.1–2.3: capacitors and dielectrics, current and resistance, and DC circuits from a physics view.

```quiz
? A parallel-plate capacitor has $A = 4\ \text{cm}^2$, $d = 0.1$ mm and $\kappa = 3$. What is $C$, in pF?
= 106.2 | 0.002 | pF
> $3(8.854\times10^{-12})(4\times10^{-4})/10^{-4}$.
```

```quiz
? How much energy does a 100 µF capacitor at 20 V store, in J?
= 0.02 | 0.001 | J
> $\tfrac12CV^2$.
```

```quiz
? Capacitors of 4 µF and 12 µF are connected in series. What is the equivalent capacitance, in µF?
= 3 | 0.001 | µF
> $1/(1/4 + 1/12)$.
```

```quiz
? What is the resistance of 50 m of 1 mm² copper ($\rho = 1.68\times10^{-8}$ Ω·m), in Ω?
= 0.84 | 0.002 | Ω
> $\rho L/A$.
```

```quiz
? A 12 V battery with 0.25 Ω internal resistance delivers 2 A. What is its terminal voltage, in V?
= 11.5 | 0.001 | V
> $12 - 2(0.25)$.
```

```quiz
? A 5000 mA·h, 3.7 V battery stores how much energy, in W·h?
= 18.5 | 0.001 | W·h
> $5 \times 3.7$.
```

```quiz
? Inserting a dielectric into a capacitor connected to a fixed voltage:
+ increases the capacitance and the stored charge
- decreases the capacitance
- leaves the charge unchanged
- reduces the stored energy
> $C$ rises by $\kappa$, so $Q = CV$ rises too.
```
