Covers Lessons 3.1–3.4: magnetic force, Biot–Savart and Ampère's laws, Faraday's law, and inductance. Use $\mu_0 = 4\pi\times10^{-7}$ T·m/A.

```quiz
? An electron moves at $2\times10^6$ m/s perpendicular to a 10 mT field. What is its orbit radius, in mm?
= 1.137 | 0.005 | mm
> $mv/(qB) = 9.11\times10^{-31} \times 2\times10^6/(1.6\times10^{-19} \times 0.01)$.
```

```quiz
? What is the field 3 cm from a long wire carrying 15 A, in µT?
= 100 | 0.001 | µT
> $\mu_0I/(2\pi r)$.
```

```quiz
? A solenoid with 3000 turns per meter carries 2 A. What is its internal field, in mT?
= 7.54 | 0.002 | mT
> $\mu_0nI$.
```

```quiz
? A 100-turn coil of 0.02 m² spins at 50 rev/s in a 0.1 T field. What is its peak emf, in V?
= 62.83 | 0.002 | V
> $NBA\omega = 100 \times 0.1 \times 0.02 \times 100\pi$.
```

```quiz
? What is the inductance of a 400-turn, 1 cm², 20 cm-long air-core solenoid, in mH?
= 0.1005 | 0.002 | mH
> $\mu_0N^2A/\ell$.
```

```quiz
? A 100 mH inductor carries 2 A. How much energy does it store, in J?
= 0.2 | 0.001 | J
> $\tfrac12LI^2$.
```

```quiz
? A magnet is pushed toward a coil. By Lenz's law, the induced current:
+ creates a field that repels the approaching magnet
- attracts the magnet toward the coil
- flows only after the magnet stops
- is zero if the coil is closed
> It opposes the increasing flux.
```
