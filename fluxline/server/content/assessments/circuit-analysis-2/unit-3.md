Covers Lessons 3.1–3.2: balanced three-phase systems, mutual inductance, and transformers.

```quiz
? A Y-connected system has 400 V line-to-line. What is the phase voltage, in V?
= 230.9 | 0.01 | V
> $400/\sqrt3 = 230.9$ V (the European 400/230 V system).
```

```quiz
? A balanced Y load of $10\angle30^\circ\ \Omega$ per phase is connected to 400 V lines. What total real power does it absorb, in kW?
= 13.86 | 0.01 | kW
> $I = 230.9/10 = 23.09$ A; $P = 3V_pI_p\cos30^\circ = 3(230.9)(23.09)(0.866) = 13.86$ kW.
```

```quiz
? A balanced Δ load has a phase current of 8 A. What is the line current, in A?
= 13.86 | 0.01 | A
> $I_L = \sqrt3 \times 8 = 13.86$ A.
```

```quiz
? Two 10 mH coils are coupled with $k = 0.8$. What is their mutual inductance, in mH?
= 8 | 0.01 | mH
> $M = k\sqrt{L_1L_2} = 0.8 \times 10 = 8$ mH.
```

```quiz
? An ideal transformer has a 1:5 turns ratio ($N_2 = 5N_1$). With 240 V on the primary, what is the secondary voltage, in V?
= 1200 | 0.01 | V
> $V_2 = nV_1 = 5 \times 240 = 1200$ V.
```

```quiz
? An ideal transformer with $n = N_2/N_1 = 0.5$ has a 4 Ω load on the secondary. What resistance does the primary source see, in Ω?
= 16 | 0.01 | Ω
> $Z_{in} = Z_L/n^2 = 4/0.25 = 16\ \Omega$.
```
