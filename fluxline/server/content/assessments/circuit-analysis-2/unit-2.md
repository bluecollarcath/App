Covers Lessons 2.1–2.4: phasors and impedance, AC circuit analysis, AC power, and power factor correction.

```quiz
? Written as a cosine phasor, $v(t) = 50\sin(377t + 30^\circ)$ V has what phase angle, in degrees?
= -60 | 0.01 | °
> $\sin\theta = \cos(\theta - 90^\circ)$, so the phasor is $50\angle{-60^\circ}$ V.
```

```quiz
? A 40 Ω resistor is in series with an inductive reactance of 30 Ω. What is the impedance magnitude, in Ω?
= 50 | 0.01 | Ω
> $|40 + j30| = 50\ \Omega$.
```

```quiz
? What is the reactance magnitude of a 47 nF capacitor at 10 kHz, in Ω?
= 338.6 | 0.01 | Ω
> $1/(2\pi \times 10^4 \times 47\times10^{-9}) = 338.6\ \Omega$.
```

```quiz
? A source $10\angle0^\circ$ V drives $\mathbf Z = 3 - j4\ \Omega$. What is the phase of the current, in degrees?
= 53.13 | 0.01 | °
> $\mathbf I = 10/(5\angle{-53.13^\circ}) = 2\angle53.13^\circ$ A. The current leads: the load is capacitive.
```

```quiz
? A load $\mathbf Z = 12 + j16\ \Omega$ is connected to 120 V rms. What real power does it absorb, in W?
= 432 | 0.01 | W
> $|\mathbf Z| = 20\ \Omega$, $I = 6$ A rms, $P = I^2R = 36 \times 12 = 432$ W.
```

```quiz
? What is the power factor of that same load ($12 + j16\ \Omega$)?
= 0.6 | 0.01 |
> $\text{pf} = R/|\mathbf Z| = 12/20 = 0.6$ (lagging).
```

```quiz
? A 5 kW load at 0.6 lagging is corrected to unity power factor. What reactive power must the capacitor supply, in kVAR?
= 6.667 | 0.01 | kVAR
> $Q_C = P\tan(\cos^{-1}0.6) = 5 \times \tfrac43 = 6.67$ kVAR.
```
