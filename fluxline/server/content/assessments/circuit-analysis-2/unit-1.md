Covers Lessons 1.1–1.2: natural and step responses of second-order RLC circuits.

```quiz
? A series LC circuit has $L = 1$ mH and $C = 1$ µF. What is its resonant frequency in hertz?
= 5033 | 0.01 | Hz
> $\omega_0 = 1/\sqrt{LC} = 31\,623$ rad/s, so $f_0 = \omega_0/2\pi = 5033$ Hz.
```

```quiz
? A series RLC has $R = 10\ \Omega$ and $L = 1$ mH. What is the neper frequency $\alpha$, in Np/s?
= 5000 | 0.01 | Np/s
> $\alpha = R/(2L) = 10/0.002 = 5000$ Np/s.
```

```quiz
? A parallel RLC has $R = 100\ \Omega$, $L = 10$ mH and $C = 1\ \mu$F. Its natural response is:
+ underdamped
- critically damped
- overdamped
- undamped
> $\omega_0 = 10\,000$ rad/s and $\alpha = 1/(2RC) = 5000$ Np/s. Since $\alpha < \omega_0$, it's underdamped.
```

```quiz
? A second-order step response has damping ratio $\zeta = 0.5$. What is its percent overshoot?
= 16.3 | 0.01 | %
> $e^{-\zeta\pi/\sqrt{1-\zeta^2}} = e^{-1.814} = 0.163$.
```

```quiz
? A series RLC has $L = 10$ mH, $C = 0.1\ \mu$F and $R = 20\ \Omega$. What is its quality factor $Q$?
= 15.81 | 0.01 |
> $\omega_0 = 31\,623$ rad/s; $Q = \omega_0L/R = 316.2/20 = 15.8$.
```

```quiz
? What series resistance makes an RLC circuit with $L = 4$ mH and $C = 10$ nF critically damped, in Ω?
= 1265 | 0.01 | Ω
> Critical damping needs $\alpha = \omega_0$, i.e. $R = 2\sqrt{L/C} = 2\sqrt{4\times10^5} = 1265\ \Omega$.
```
