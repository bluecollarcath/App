```quiz
? What is the DC Fourier coefficient $c_0$ of $x(t) = 2 + \cos(100t)$?
= 2 | 0.001 |
> $c_0$ is the average value.
```

```quiz
? What is $|X(\omega)|$ at $\omega = 4$ rad/s for $x(t) = e^{-3t}u(t)$?
= 0.2 | 0.01 |
> $1/|3 + j4| = 1/5$.
```

```quiz
? A rectangular pulse lasts 0.5 ms. Where is the first null of its spectrum, in Hz?
= 2000 | 0.001 | Hz
> $f = 1/T = 2$ kHz.
```

```quiz
? A 3-point moving average has $H(e^{j\omega}) = \frac13(1 + 2\cos\omega)$ (up to a delay). What is its magnitude at $\omega = 2\pi/3$?
= 0 | 0.001 |
> $1 + 2\cos(2\pi/3) = 1 - 1 = 0$: that frequency is completely blocked.
```

```quiz
? Audio is sampled at 48 kHz. What frequency, in Hz, corresponds to $\omega = \pi/4$ rad/sample?
= 6000 | 0.001 | Hz
> $f = \omega f_s/2\pi = 48\,000/8 = 6$ kHz.
```
