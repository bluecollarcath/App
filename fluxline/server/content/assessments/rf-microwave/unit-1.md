Covers Lessons 1.1–1.3: S-parameters, matching networks, and couplers, dividers and filters.

```quiz
? A filter has $|S_{21}| = 0.8$. What is its insertion loss, in dB?
= 1.938 | 0.005 | dB
> $-20\log_{10}0.8$.
```

```quiz
? A lossless reciprocal two-port has $|S_{11}| = 0.3$. What is $|S_{21}|$?
= 0.9539 | 0.002 |
> $\sqrt{1 - 0.09}$.
```

```quiz
? A port has $|S_{11}| = 0.2$. What is its return loss, in dB?
= 13.98 | 0.005 | dB
> $-20\log_{10}0.2$.
```

```quiz
? An L-network matches 300 Ω to 50 Ω. What is its Q?
= 2.236 | 0.002 |
> $\sqrt{300/50 - 1} = \sqrt5$.
```

```quiz
? What shunt reactance does that network need, in Ω?
= 134.2 | 0.002 | Ω
> $R_{high}/Q = 300/2.236$.
```

```quiz
? What is the through-loss of an ideal 20 dB directional coupler, in dB?
= 0.04365 | 0.01 | dB
> $-10\log_{10}(1 - 0.01)$.
```

- sets the division ratio
