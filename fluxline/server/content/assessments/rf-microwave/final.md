The final exam covers all three units of RF & Microwave Engineering. Numeric answers are accepted within about 1%.

```quiz
? A network has $|S_{11}| = 0.1$. What is its return loss, in dB?
= 20 | 0.001 | dB
> $-20\log_{10}0.1$.
```

```quiz
? What is the VSWR for $|\Gamma| = 0.2$?
= 1.5 | 0.001 |
> $1.2/0.8$.
```

```quiz
? An amplifier has $|S_{21}| = 10$. What is its gain in a 50 Ω system, in dB?
= 20 | 0.001 | dB
> $20\log_{10}10$.
```

```quiz
? An L-network matches 1250 Ω down to 50 Ω. What is its Q?
= 4.899 | 0.002 |
> $\sqrt{25 - 1}$.
```

```quiz
? What series reactance does that network need, in Ω?
= 244.9 | 0.002 | Ω
> $QR_{low}$.
```

```quiz
? A series reactance of 50 Ω at 2.4 GHz is realized with what inductance, in nH?
= 3.316 | 0.005 | nH
> $50/(2\pi \times 2.4\times10^9)$.
```

```quiz
? What arm impedance does a Wilkinson divider for a 75 Ω system need, in Ω?
= 106.1 | 0.002 | Ω
> $\sqrt2 \times 75$.
```

```quiz
? A coupler has 30 dB coupling and 55 dB isolation. What is its directivity, in dB?
= 25 | 0.001 | dB
> $55 - 30$.
```

```quiz
? A component has a noise temperature of 290 K. What is its noise figure, in dB?
= 3.01 | 0.002 | dB
> $F = 2$.
```

```quiz
? Stage 1: F = 1.4, G = 50. Stage 2: F = 11. What is the cascade noise factor?
= 1.6 | 0.001 |
> $1.4 + 10/50$.
```

```quiz
? A 25 kHz-wide receiver has NF = 6 dB and needs 10 dB SNR. What is its sensitivity, in dBm?
= -114 | 0.002 | dBm
> $-174 + 44 + 6 + 10$.
```

```quiz
? A synthesizer has $N = 1000$. How much is the reference phase noise multiplied inside the loop, in dB?
= 60 | 0.001 | dB
> $20\log_{10}1000$.
```

```quiz
? An amplifier with IIP3 = +10 dBm is driven by two −20 dBm tones. How far below the tones is the IM3 product, in dB?
= 60 | 0.001 | dB
> $P_{IM3} = -60 - 20 = -80$ dBm; $-20 - (-80) = 60$ dB.
```

```quiz
? $P_t = 23$ dBm, $G_t = G_r = 6$ dBi, path loss 110 dB, 1 dB other losses. Sensitivity is −90 dBm. What is the margin, in dB?
= 14 | 0.001 | dB
> $P_r = -76$ dBm.
```

- the antenna

> Cascade IIP3 is dominated by the stages after the gain.
