Covers Lessons 2.1–2.3: microwave amplifier design, noise, and mixers and oscillators.

```quiz
? A transistor has $|S_{21}| = 5$. What is its gain in a 50 Ω system, in dB?
= 13.98 | 0.005 | dB
> $20\log_{10}5$.
```

```quiz
? With $|S_{11}| = 0.7$, $|S_{21}| = 5$ and $|S_{22}| = 0.6$ (unilateral), what is $G_{TU,max}$, in dB?
= 18.84 | 0.005 | dB
> $10\log_{10}\big(25/(0.51 \times 0.64)\big)$.
```

```quiz
? An amplifier has NF = 1.5 dB. What is its noise temperature, in K?
= 119.6 | 0.005 | K
> $(10^{0.15} - 1) \times 290$.
```

```quiz
? An LNA (NF 2 dB, gain 12 dB) feeds a stage with NF 9 dB. What is the cascade noise figure, in dB?
= 3.06 | 0.005 | dB
> $F = 1.585 + 6.94/15.85 = 2.02$.
```

```quiz
? A 10 MHz-wide receiver has NF = 4 dB and needs 8 dB SNR. What is its sensitivity, in dBm?
= -92 | 0.002 | dBm
> $-174 + 70 + 4 + 8$.
```

```quiz
? A PLL with a 0.5 MHz reference must produce 2.4 GHz. What is $N$?
= 4800 | 0.001 |
> $2400/0.5$.
```

```quiz
? A transistor has $K > 1$ and $|\Delta| < 1$. It is:
+ unconditionally stable
- potentially unstable
- certain to oscillate
- unilateral
> The Rollett conditions guarantee stability for all passive terminations.
```
