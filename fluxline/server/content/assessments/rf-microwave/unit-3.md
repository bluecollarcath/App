Covers Lesson 3.1: link budgets, receiver architectures, and linearity.

```quiz
? A 900 MHz link spans 5 km. What is the free-space path loss, in dB?
= 105.5 | 0.002 | dB
> $20\log_{10}(4\pi \times 5000/0.333)$.
```

```quiz
? That link uses 27 dBm, an 8 dBi transmit antenna, a 2 dBi receive antenna and 2 dB of cable loss. What is $P_r$, in dBm?
= -70.51 | 0.005 | dBm
> $27 + 8 + 2 - 105.5 - 2$.
```

```quiz
? With IIP3 = −15 dBm and two −35 dBm tones, what is the input-referred IM3 level, in dBm?
= -75 | 0.001 | dBm
> $3(-35) - 2(-15)$.
```

```quiz
? IIP3 = −15 dBm and the noise floor is −105 dBm. What is the SFDR, in dB?
= 60 | 0.001 | dB
> $\tfrac23(90)$.
```

```quiz
? Third-order intermodulation products from tones at 100 and 101 MHz appear at:
+ 99 and 102 MHz
- 1 and 201 MHz
- 200 and 202 MHz
- 300 and 303 MHz
> $2f_1 - f_2$ and $2f_2 - f_1$.
```
