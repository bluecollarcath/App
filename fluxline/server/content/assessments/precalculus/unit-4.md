Covers Lessons 4.1–4.3: right triangles, the Pythagorean theorem, sine, cosine and tangent, radians; the unit circle and trig identities; sine waves, frequency, angular frequency, phase and RMS.

```quiz
? A right triangle has shorter sides 8 and 15. How long is the hypotenuse?
= 17 | 0.001 |
> $64 + 225 = 289$, and $\sqrt{289} = 17$.
```

```quiz
? A right triangle has hypotenuse 13 and one side 5. How long is the other side?
= 12 | 0.001 |
> $169 - 25 = 144$, and $\sqrt{144} = 12$.
```

```quiz
? In the 8-15-17 right triangle, what is the sine of the angle opposite the side of length 8?
= 0.4706 | 0.002 |
> $\dfrac{\text{opposite}}{\text{hypotenuse}} = \dfrac{8}{17} \approx 0.4706$.
```

```quiz
? A 5 m ladder leans against a wall at 60° to the ground. How high up the wall does it reach, in m?
= 4.33 | 0.002 | m
> The height is the side opposite the 60° angle: $5 \sin 60° \approx 4.33$ m.
```

```quiz
? A right triangle has opposite side 1 and hypotenuse 2. What is the angle, in degrees?
= 30 | 0.001 | °
> $\sin\theta = 0.5$, so $\theta = \sin^{-1}(0.5) = 30°$.
```

```quiz
? How many radians is 135°?
= 2.3562 | 0.001 | rad
> $135 \times \dfrac{\pi}{180} = \dfrac{3\pi}{4} \approx 2.356$.
```

```quiz
? How many degrees is $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$ radians?
= 120 | 0.001 | °
> $\dfrac{2\pi}{3} \times \dfrac{180}{\pi} = 120°$.
```

```quiz
? What is $\cos 240°$?
= -0.5 | 0.001 |
> Reference angle 60°; at 240° the point is left of center, so cosine is negative: $-\cos 60° = -0.5$.
```

```quiz
? What is $\sin 300°$?
= -0.866 | 0.002 |
> Reference angle 60°; at 300° the point is below the axis: $-\sin 60° \approx -0.866$.
```

```quiz
? What is the period of a 400 Hz signal, in ms?
= 2.5 | 0.001 | ms
> $T = \dfrac{1}{400} = 0.0025$ s $= 2.5$ ms.
```

```quiz
? What is the angular frequency of a 1 kHz signal, in rad/s?
= 6283.2 | 0.002 | rad/s
> $\omega = 2\pi \times 1000 \approx 6283$ rad/s.
```

```quiz
? A signal is $v(t) = 20\sin(314.16\,t)$. What is its frequency, in Hz?
= 50 | 0.002 | Hz
> $f = \dfrac{\omega}{2\pi} = \dfrac{314.16}{6.2832} \approx 50$ Hz.
```

```quiz
? Compared with $\sin(\omega t)$, the wave $\sin(\omega t - 30°)$:
+ lags by 30° (is shifted later)
- leads by 30° (is shifted earlier)
- has a 30% larger amplitude
- has a higher frequency
> Negative phase shifts the wave later: it lags.
```

```quiz
? Mains voltage in Europe is 230 V RMS. What is the peak voltage, in V?
= 325.3 | 0.002 | V
> $\sqrt{2} \times 230 \approx 325$ V.
```
