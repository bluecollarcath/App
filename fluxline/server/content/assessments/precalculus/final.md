Covers the whole course: algebra, exponents and prefixes, equations and quadratics; functions, lines, systems and inverses; exponentials and logarithms; trigonometry and sine waves; and the first ideas of calculus. Fractions are accepted as answers.

```quiz
? What is $(-3)^2 - 4 \times 2$?
= 1 | 0.001 |
> $9 - 8 = 1$.
```

```quiz
? What is $\dfrac{1}{3} + \dfrac{1}{6}$?
= 0.5 | 0.001 |
> $\dfrac{2}{6} + \dfrac{1}{6} = \dfrac{3}{6} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.
```

```quiz
? Solve $7 - 2x = 3x - 8$.
= 3 | 0.001 |
> Add $2x$ and $8$ to both sides: $15 = 5x$, so $x = 3$.
```

```quiz
? Solve $2x^2 - 4x - 3 = 0$. What is the **positive** solution, to two decimal places?
= 2.58 | 0.003 |
> $b^2 - 4ac = 16 + 24 = 40$, so $x = \dfrac{4 + \sqrt{40}}{4} \approx 2.58$.
```

```quiz
? What is the slope of the line through $(0, 2)$ and $(5, 12)$?
= 2 | 0.001 |
> $\dfrac{12 - 2}{5 - 0} = 2$.
```


```quiz
? Let $f(x) = x^2$ and $g(x) = x - 5$. What is $f(g(8))$?
```

```quiz
? Which statement is true?
+ $\ln(xy) = \ln x + \ln y$
- $\ln(x + y) = \ln x + \ln y$
- $\ln(x^3) = (\ln x)^3$
- $\ln 0 = 1$
> A log turns a product into a sum. ($\ln 0$ doesn't exist, and $\ln 1 = 0$.)
```

```quiz
? Solve $e^{-t/2} = 0.25$ for $t$, to two decimal places.
= 2.77 | 0.003 |
> $-\dfrac{t}{2} = \ln 0.25$, so $t = -2\ln 0.25 = 2\ln 4 \approx 2.77$.
```

```quiz
? A right triangle has shorter sides 9 and 12. How long is the hypotenuse?
= 15 | 0.001 |
> $81 + 144 = 225$, and $\sqrt{225} = 15$.
```

```quiz
? A 6 m ladder leans at 75° to the ground. How high up the wall does it reach, in m?
= 5.80 | 0.003 | m
> $6\sin 75° \approx 5.80$ m.
```

```quiz
? How many radians is 315°?
= 5.4978 | 0.001 | rad
> $315 \times \dfrac{\pi}{180} = \dfrac{7\pi}{4} \approx 5.498$.
```

```quiz
? What is the angular frequency of a 50 Hz wave, in rad/s?
= 314.16 | 0.002 | rad/s
> $\omega = 2\pi \times 50 \approx 314.16$ rad/s.
```

```quiz
? What is the average rate of change of $f(x) = 3x^2$ from $x = 1$ to $x = 3$?
= 12 | 0.001 |
> $\dfrac{27 - 3}{3 - 1} = \dfrac{24}{2} = 12$.
```

```quiz
? What is $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 5} \frac{x^2 - 25}{x - 5}$?
= 10 | 0.001 |
> $\dfrac{(x - 5)(x + 5)}{x - 5} = x + 5 \to 10$.
```

```quiz
? A steady 5 mA current flows for 2 minutes. How much charge flows, in C?
= 0.6 | 0.001 | C
> Convert first: 2 minutes is 120 s, and 5 mA is 0.005 A. Charge $= 0.005 \times 120 = 0.6$ C.
```
