The final exam covers all three units of Senior Design & Professional Practice. Numeric answers are accepted within about 1%.

```quiz
? Weighted decision matrix: option X scores 8 (weight 0.5), 6 (weight 0.3) and 4 (weight 0.2). What is its total?
= 6.6 | 0.001 |
> $4 + 1.8 + 0.8$.
```

```quiz
? Tasks: A (3), B (4, after A), C (6, after A), D (2, after B and C), in weeks. What is the critical-path length, in weeks?
= 11 | 0.001 | weeks
> A–C–D.
```

```quiz
? A task is estimated at 1, 3 and 11 days. What is the PERT standard deviation, in days?
= 1.667 | 0.002 | days
> $(11 - 1)/6$.
```

```quiz
? A risk has a 10% chance of costing 200,000 dollars. What is its exposure, in dollars?
```
