Covers Lessons 2.1–2.3: engineering ethics, electrical safety and codes, and technical communication.

```quiz
? A worker with 1 kΩ of body resistance touches 277 V. What current flows, in mA?
= 277 | 0.001 | mA
> $277/1000$ A, far into the lethal range.
```

```quiz
? A 20 A load at 120 V sits 75 ft (one way) from the panel on 12 AWG (1.59 Ω/1000 ft). What is the voltage drop, in V?
= 4.77 | 0.002 | V
> $20 \times 2(75)(0.00159)$.
```

```quiz
? A measured gain is 9.6 versus a design value of 10. What is the percent error?
= 4 | 0.001 | %
> $0.4/10$.
```

```quiz
? Which NSPE canon comes first?
+ Hold paramount the safety, health and welfare of the public
- Act as a faithful agent of the employer
- Avoid deceptive acts
- Issue public statements truthfully
> Public safety is the first duty.
```

```quiz
? A GFCI protects people by:
+ tripping when the hot and neutral currents differ by a few milliamps
- tripping on overcurrent only
- lowering the supply voltage
- detecting arcing in the wiring
> A difference means current is leaking, possibly through a person.
```
