Covers Lessons 3.1–3.2: FE exam review across the curriculum.

```quiz
? A 10 V source with 2 kΩ in series feeds 8 kΩ to ground. What is the open-circuit voltage across the 8 kΩ, in V?
= 8 | 0.001 | V
> $10 \times 8/10$.
```

```quiz
? A capacitor charges through 4.7 kΩ; $C = 100\ \mu$F. What is the time constant, in s?
= 0.47 | 0.001 | s
> $RC$.
```

```quiz
? A 15 kW load at 0.75 pf lagging needs how much capacitive kVAR for unity pf?
= 13.23 | 0.005 | kVAR
> $15\tan(\cos^{-1}0.75) = 15 \times 0.882$.
```

```quiz
? A 4160 V three-phase motor draws 100 A at 0.88 pf. What is its real power, in kW?
= 634.1 | 0.002 | kW
> $\sqrt3(4160)(100)(0.88)$.
```

```quiz
? What is 8-bit two's-complement 0xF6 as a signed decimal number?
= -10 | 0.001 |
> $246 - 256$.
```

```quiz
? What is the present worth of 2000 dollars due in 3 years at 8%, in dollars?
= 1587.7 | 0.002 |
> $2000/1.08^3$.
```
