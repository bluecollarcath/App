Covers Lessons 2.1–2.2: transmission line parameters and line models.

```quiz
? A line has phase spacings of 4, 4 and 8 m. What is $D_{eq}$, in m?
= 5.04 | 0.002 | m
> $\sqrt[3]{128}$.
```

```quiz
? With $D_{eq} = 5.04$ m and conductor GMR 1.2 cm, what is the inductance per phase, in mH/km?
= 1.208 | 0.005 | mH/km
> $2\times10^{-7}\ln(504/1.2)$.
```

```quiz
? What is the SIL of a 765 kV line with $Z_c = 280\ \Omega$, in MW?
= 2090 | 0.002 | MW
> $765^2/280$.
```

```quiz
? A medium line has $|A| = 0.95$. At no load with 1.0 pu sent, what is the receiving-end voltage, in pu?
= 1.0526 | 0.002 | pu
> $1/0.95$: the Ferranti effect.
```

```quiz
? A line with $X = 0.25$ pu connects buses at 1.05 and 1.0 pu. What is its maximum transfer, in pu?
= 4.2 | 0.001 | pu
> $V_SV_R/X$.
```

```quiz
? Which line model is adequate for a 40 km, 60 Hz line?
+ the short-line model (series impedance only)
- the long-line model with hyperbolic functions
- the nominal π model is required
- the line can be ignored entirely
> Lines under about 80 km have negligible shunt capacitance.
```
