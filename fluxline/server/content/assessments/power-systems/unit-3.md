Covers Lessons 3.1–3.3: power flow, symmetrical faults, and symmetrical components.

```quiz
? Two 1.0 pu buses are joined by $X = 0.25$ pu and carry 0.8 pu. What is the angle difference, in degrees?
= 11.54 | 0.005 | °
> $\sin\delta = 0.8 \times 0.25 = 0.2$.
```

```quiz
? A bus with line admittances $-j6$, $-j8$ and $-j3$ to its neighbors has what $|Y_{ii}|$?
= 17 | 0.001 |
> The sum of connected admittances.
```

```quiz
? A bus has a Thévenin impedance of 0.125 pu. What is its three-phase fault current, in pu?
= 8 | 0.001 | pu
> $1/0.125$.
```

```quiz
? What is that bus's short-circuit MVA on a 100 MVA base?
= 800 | 0.001 | MVA
> $100/0.125$.
```

```quiz
? $Z_1 = Z_2 = j0.1$ and $Z_0 = j0.05$ pu. What is the SLG fault current, in pu?

```
