Covers Lessons 4.1–4.2: power system stability and grid integration of renewables.

```quiz
? A generator with $P_{max} = 1.8$ pu delivers 1.0 pu. What is its operating angle, in degrees?
= 33.75 | 0.005 | °
> $\sin^{-1}(1/1.8)$.
```

```quiz
? For that machine, after a terminal fault cleared with the original network restored, what is the critical clearing angle, in degrees?
= 74.97 | 0.005 | °
> $\cos\delta_{cr} = (\pi - 2\delta_0)\sin\delta_0 - \cos\delta_0$.
```

```quiz
? 800 MW of generation trips in a 40,000 MVA system with $H = 4$ s (60 Hz). What is the initial ROCOF, in Hz/s?
= 0.15 | 0.001 | Hz/s
> $800(60)/(2 \times 4 \times 40\,000)$.
```

```quiz
? A 200 MW unit with 5% droop sees frequency fall 0.3 Hz (60 Hz). How much does its output rise, in MW?
= 20 | 0.001 | MW
> $(0.3/60)/0.05 \times 200$.
```

```quiz
? The equal-area criterion says a machine stays in synchronism if:
+ the decelerating area after clearing at least equals the accelerating area during the fault
- the rotor angle stays below 30°
- the fault is cleared in less than one cycle
- the mechanical power equals the electrical power at all times
> Kinetic energy gained must be returned before the unstable equilibrium.
```

