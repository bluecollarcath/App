The final exam covers all four units of Power Systems. Numeric answers are accepted within about 1%.

```quiz
? A balanced load draws 50 A at 13.8 kV line-to-line and 0.9 pf. What is its real power, in MW?
= 1.0756 | 0.005 | MW
> $\sqrt3(13\,800)(50)(0.9)$.
```

```quiz
? What is the impedance base for 100 MVA at 69 kV, in Ω?
= 47.61 | 0.002 | Ω
> $69^2/100$.
```

```quiz
? A 10 Ω line in that zone is how many per-unit?
= 0.21 | 0.005 |
> $10/47.61$.
```

```quiz
? A 50 MVA generator has $X''_d = 0.18$ pu on its rating. What is it on a 100 MVA base?
= 0.36 | 0.001 |
> $0.18 \times 100/50$.
```

```quiz
? A transformer has 8 kW core loss and 32 kW full-load copper loss. At what load fraction is its efficiency maximum?
= 0.5 | 0.001 |
> $\sqrt{8/32}$.
```

```quiz
? A conductor of 1.4 cm radius has what GMR, in cm?
= 1.0903 | 0.002 | cm
> $0.7788 \times 1.4$.
```

```quiz
? What is the SIL of a 230 kV line with $Z_c = 380\ \Omega$, in MW?
= 139.2 | 0.005 | MW
> $230^2/380$.
```

```quiz
? Using the DC power flow, what flows on a line with $X = 0.08$ pu between angles of 12° and 7°, in pu?
= 1.091 | 0.005 | pu
> $(5° = 0.0873\ \text{rad})/0.08$.
```

```quiz
? A generator ($X''_d = 0.2$) feeds a fault through a transformer ($X = 0.08$) on a 100 MVA base. What is the fault current, in pu?
= 3.571 | 0.002 | pu
> $1/0.28$.
```

```quiz
? $Z_1 = Z_2 = j0.12$ and $Z_0 = j0.06$ pu. What is the SLG fault current, in pu?
= 10 | 0.001 | pu
> $3/0.3$.
```

```quiz
? A grounding reactor of 0.1 pu adds how much to the zero-sequence impedance, in pu?
= 0.3 | 0.001 | pu
> $3Z_n$.
```

```quiz
? A machine has $H = 6$ s, $P_{max} = 2$ pu and $\delta_0 = 30°$ at 60 Hz. What is its swing frequency, in Hz?
= 1.174 | 0.005 | Hz
> $\omega_n = \sqrt{377 \times 2 \times 0.866/12} = 7.38$ rad/s.
```

```quiz
? A machine with $H = 4$ s and $P_m = 0.9$ pu (60 Hz) may advance 0.7 rad during a terminal fault. What is its critical clearing time, in s?
= 0.1817 | 0.005 | s
> $\sqrt{4(4)(0.7)/(377 \times 0.9)}$.
```

```quiz
? Losing 1200 MW in a 60,000 MVA, 60 Hz system with $H = 3$ s gives what initial ROCOF, in Hz/s?
= 0.2 | 0.001 | Hz/s
> $1200(60)/(2 \times 3 \times 60\,000)$.
```
