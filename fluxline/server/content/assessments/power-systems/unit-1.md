Covers Lessons 1.1–1.3: three-phase power, the per-unit system, and power transformers.

```quiz
? A feeder operates at 12.47 kV line-to-line. What is its line-to-neutral voltage, in kV?
= 7.2 | 0.002 | kV
> $12.47/\sqrt3$.
```

```quiz
? A 480 V three-phase motor draws 100 A at 0.85 pf. What real power does it draw, in kW?
= 70.67 | 0.005 | kW
> $\sqrt3(480)(100)(0.85)$.
```

```quiz
? What is the impedance base for 100 MVA and 345 kV, in Ω?
= 1190.25 | 0.001 | Ω
> $345^2/100$.
```

```quiz
? What is the current base for 100 MVA at 345 kV, in A?
= 167.3 | 0.005 | A
> $10^8/(\sqrt3 \times 345\,000)$.
```

```quiz
? A 250 MVA transformer has 0.1 pu impedance on its rating at 22 kV. What is it on a 100 MVA, 20 kV base?
= 0.0484 | 0.005 |
> $0.1(100/250)(22/20)^2$.
```

```quiz
? A transformer has $R = 0.015$ and $X = 0.07$ pu. Estimate its full-load voltage regulation at 0.8 pf lagging, in %.
= 5.4 | 0.002 | %
> $0.015(0.8) + 0.07(0.6) = 0.054$.
```

```quiz
? Which transformer connection traps zero-sequence currents and introduces a 30° phase shift?
+ Δ–Y
- Y–Y
- autotransformer
- Δ–Δ
> The Δ winding provides a circulating path for zero sequence.
```
