Covers Lessons 4.1–4.4: partial derivatives and gradients, multiple integrals, line and surface integrals, and the divergence and Stokes theorems.

```quiz
? $f(x, y) = x^2y^3$. What is $\partial f/\partial x$ at $(2, 1)$?
= 4 | 0.001 |
> $2xy^3$.
```

```quiz
? $V = 3x + 4y$ volts. What is $|\mathbf E|$, in V/m?
= 5 | 0.001 | V/m
> $|\nabla V|$.
```

```quiz
? A sphere of radius 2 has uniform density 1. What is its total mass?
= 33.51 | 0.002 |
> $\tfrac43\pi(8)$.
```

```quiz
? $\mathbf F = \nabla(xyz)$. What is its line integral from $(0, 0, 0)$ to $(1, 2, 3)$?
= 6 | 0.001 |
> Path-independent: $\phi(1, 2, 3) - \phi(0, 0, 0)$.
```

```quiz
? What is the divergence of $\mathbf F = (x^2, y^2, z^2)$ at $(1, 1, 1)$?
= 6 | 0.001 |
> $2x + 2y + 2z$.
```

```quiz
? A field with divergence 2 everywhere: what is its outward flux through a sphere of radius 3?
= 226.2 | 0.002 |
> $2 \times \tfrac43\pi(27) = 72\pi$.
```

```quiz
? A field with zero curl everywhere is:
+ conservative: its line integrals are path-independent
- divergence-free
- zero everywhere
- a magnetic field
> Stokes' theorem gives zero circulation around every loop.
```
