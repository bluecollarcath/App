Covers Lessons 3.1–3.3: sequences and series, Taylor series, and complex numbers.

```quiz
? What is $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^\infty(0.6)^n$?
= 2.5 | 0.001 |
> $1/(1 - 0.6)$.
```

```quiz
? What is the radius of convergence of $\sum x^n/3^n$?
= 3 | 0.001 |
> Geometric with ratio $x/3$.
```

```quiz
? Using $e^x \approx 1 + x + x^2/2$, estimate $e^{0.3}$.
= 1.345 | 0.001 |
> $1 + 0.3 + 0.045$.
```

```quiz
? Linearize $\sqrt x$ at $x = 9$ to estimate $\sqrt{9.6}$.
= 3.1 | 0.001 |
> $3 + 0.6/6$.
```

