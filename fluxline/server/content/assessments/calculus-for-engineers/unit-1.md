? The charge in a wire is $q(t) = t^3 - 2t$ C. What is the current at $t = 2$ s, in A?

```quiz
? $f(t) = 3t^2e^{-t}$. What is $f'(0)$?
= 0 | 0 |
> $f' = (6t - 3t^2)e^{-t}$.
```

```quiz
? What is $\dfrac{d}{dx}(x^2 + 1)^4$ at $x = 1$?
= 64 | 0.001 |
> $4(x^2 + 1)^3 \cdot 2x = 4(8)(2)$.
```

```quiz
? An RC circuit has $\tau = 5$ ms. How long does it take to reach 90% of its final value, in ms?
= 11.51 | 0.002 | ms
> $\tau\ln10$.
```

```quiz
? An inductor current decays with $\tau = 4$ ms. How long until it falls to 25% of its initial value, in ms?

```
