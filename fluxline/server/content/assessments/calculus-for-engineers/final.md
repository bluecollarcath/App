The final exam covers all four units of Calculus for Engineers. Numeric answers are accepted within about 1%.

```quiz
? The voltage on a 2 µF capacitor is $v = 10\sin(500t)$ V. What is the peak current, in mA?
= 10 | 0.001 | mA
> $C\omega V_p = 2\times10^{-6} \times 500 \times 10$.
```

```quiz
? What is $\dfrac{d}{dx}\dfrac{x}{x + 1}$ at $x = 1$?
= 0.25 | 0.001 |
> $1/(x + 1)^2$.
```

```quiz
? A transient rises with $\tau = 3$ ms. How long until it reaches 50% of final, in ms?
= 2.079 | 0.002 | ms
> $\tau\ln2$.
```

```quiz
? What is the maximum of $f(t) = 4te^{-t}$ for $t \ge 0$?
= 1.4715 | 0.002 |
> At $t = 1$: $4/e$.
```

```quiz
? What is $\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/2}\cos t\,dt$?
= 1 | 0.001 |
> $\sin(\pi/2) - \sin0$.
```

```quiz
? What is $\displaystyle\int_0^1 xe^{-x}\,dx$?
= 0.2642 | 0.002 |
> $-(x + 1)e^{-x}\big|_0^1 = 1 - 2/e$.
```

```quiz
? What is the average of a full-wave rectified sine with a 12 V peak, in V?
= 7.639 | 0.002 | V
> $2(12)/\pi$.
```

```quiz
? A pulse $v = 5e^{-t/0.01}$ V drives 10 Ω. What total energy is delivered, in mJ?
= 12.5 | 0.001 | mJ
> $V_0^2\tau/2R = 25 \times 0.01/20$.
```

```quiz
? What is $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^\infty 4(0.75)^n$?
= 16 | 0.001 |
> $4/0.25$.
```

```quiz
? Using the cubic Taylor approximation, estimate $\sin(0.5)$.
= 0.4792 | 0.002 |
> $0.5 - 0.125/6$.
```

```quiz
? What is the real part of $(1 + j)^2$?
= 0 | 0 |
> $1 + 2j + j^2 = 2j$.
```

```quiz
? What is $|e^{j1.2}|$?
= 1 | 0.001 |
> Every $e^{j\theta}$ has magnitude 1.
```

```quiz
? $P = V^2/R$ with $V$ uncertain by 2% and $R$ by 1%. What is the worst-case uncertainty in $P$, in %?
= 5 | 0.001 | %
> $2(2\%) + 1\%$.
```

```quiz
? What is $\displaystyle\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^2 r\,dr\,d\theta$?
= 12.566 | 0.002 |
> The area of a disk of radius 2: $4\pi$.
```

```quiz
? By Green's theorem, what is $\oint(-y\,dx + x\,dy)$ around a circle of radius 2, counterclockwise?
= 25.13 | 0.002 |
> $2 \times 4\pi$.
```
