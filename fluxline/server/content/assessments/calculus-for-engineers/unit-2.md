Covers Lessons 2.1–2.4: definite integrals, integration techniques, average and RMS values, and improper integrals.

```quiz
? What is $\displaystyle\int_0^1 x^2\,dx$?
= 0.3333 | 0.002 |
> $1/3$.
```

```quiz
? What is $\displaystyle\int_0^2 e^{x}\,dx$?
= 6.389 | 0.002 |
> $e^2 - 1$.
```

```quiz
? A constant 3 mA charges a 1 µF capacitor from 0 V for 2 ms. What voltage results, in V?
= 6 | 0.001 | V
> $It/C$.
```

```quiz
? What is the RMS value of a sinusoid with a 10 V peak, in V?
= 7.071 | 0.002 | V
> $10/\sqrt2$.
```

```quiz
? A 6 V pulse train has a 36% duty cycle. What is its RMS value, in V?
= 3.6 | 0.001 | V
> $6\sqrt{0.36}$.
```

```quiz
? A signal is 5 V DC plus 3 V RMS of AC. What is its total RMS value, in V?
= 5.831 | 0.002 | V
> $\sqrt{34}$.
```

```quiz
? What is $\displaystyle\int_0^\infty 2e^{-4t}\,dt$?
= 0.5 | 0.001 |
> $2/4$.
```
