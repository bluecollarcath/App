Covers Lessons 2.1–2.3: Newton's laws and free-body diagrams, weight, normal force, friction, tension and inclines, centripetal force, universal gravitation and orbits. Use $g = 9.8$ m/s², $G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11}$ N·m²/kg² and $GM_{\text{Earth}} = 3.986 \times 10^{14}$ m³/s².

```quiz
? A 45 N net force acts on a 15 kg object. What is its acceleration, in m/s²?
= 3 | 0.001 | m/s²
> $a = 45/15 = 3.0$ m/s².
```

```quiz
? An 80 kg person stands on a scale in an elevator accelerating upward at 1.5 m/s². What does the scale read, in N?
= 904 | 0.002 | N
> $N = m(g + a) = 80(11.3) = 904$ N.
```

```quiz
? A 30 kg crate rests on a floor with $\mu_s = 0.40$. What is the largest horizontal push it can withstand without moving, in N?
= 117.6 | 0.002 | N
> $\mu_s mg = 0.40 \times 30 \times 9.8 = 118$ N.
```

```quiz
? You push that 30 kg crate with 150 N, and once it's sliding $\mu_k = 0.30$. What is its acceleration, in m/s²?
= 2.06 | 0.005 | m/s²
> Friction: $0.30 \times 294 = 88.2$ N. $a = (150 - 88.2)/30 = 2.06$ m/s².
```

```quiz
? A box slides down a 20° ramp with $\mu_k = 0.10$. What is its acceleration, in m/s²?
= 2.431 | 0.005 | m/s²
> $a = 9.8(\sin 20° - 0.10\cos 20°) = 9.8(0.342 - 0.094) = 2.43$ m/s².
```

```quiz
? An 8.0 kg block rests on a 35° incline. What is the normal force on it, in N?
= 64.22 | 0.005 | N
> $N = mg\cos 35° = 78.4 \times 0.819 = 64.2$ N.
```

```quiz
? A 4.0 kg mass and a 6.0 kg mass hang on opposite sides of a frictionless pulley. What is the tension in the string, in N?
= 47.04 | 0.003 | N
> $a = \dfrac{(6 - 4)g}{10} = 1.96$ m/s², and $T = 4.0(9.8 + 1.96) = 47.0$ N.
```

```quiz
? A 5.0 kg cart on a frictionless table is pulled by a string over a pulley to a hanging 3.0 kg mass. What is the acceleration, in m/s²?
= 3.675 | 0.005 | m/s²
> $a = \dfrac{3.0 \times 9.8}{5.0 + 3.0} = 3.68$ m/s².
```

```quiz
? A block on a ramp has $\mu_s = 0.35$. At what angle does it begin to slide, in degrees?
= 19.29 | 0.005 | °
> $\theta = \tan^{-1}(0.35) = 19.3°$.
```

```quiz
? What is the fastest a car can take a flat curve of radius 80 m with $\mu_s = 0.50$, in m/s?
= 19.8 | 0.005 | m/s
> $v = \sqrt{0.50 \times 9.8 \times 80} = 19.8$ m/s.
```

```quiz
? A 0.20 kg ball on a 0.80 m string moves in a horizontal circle at 3.0 m/s. What is the tension, in N?
= 2.25 | 0.002 | N
> $T = mv^2/r = 0.20 \times 9/0.80 = 2.25$ N.
```

```quiz
? What is the gravitational acceleration at the surface of Mars ($M = 6.42 \times 10^{23}$ kg, $R = 3.39 \times 10^6$ m), in m/s²?
= 3.728 | 0.005 | m/s²
> $g = GM/R^2 = \dfrac{(6.674 \times 10^{-11})(6.42 \times 10^{23})}{(3.39 \times 10^6)^2} = 3.73$ m/s².
```

```quiz
? What is the speed of a satellite in a circular orbit of radius $7.0 \times 10^6$ m, in m/s?
= 7546 | 0.005 | m/s
> $v = \sqrt{GM/r} = \sqrt{3.986 \times 10^{14}/7.0 \times 10^6} = 7546$ m/s.
```

```quiz
? A book rests on a level table. Which pair of forces is a Newton's third-law pair?
+ The table pushing up on the book, and the book pushing down on the table
- The book's weight and the table's normal force on the book
- The book's weight and the table's weight
- Friction and the normal force
> Third-law pairs act on different objects. The weight and the normal force both act on the book.
```
