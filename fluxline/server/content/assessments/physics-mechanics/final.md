Covers the whole course: vectors and kinematics, projectiles and circular motion, Newton's laws, friction, inclines and gravity, work, energy, power and momentum, rotation, torque and angular momentum, and oscillations, resonance and the RLC analogy. Use $g = 9.8$ m/s² and $GM_{\text{Earth}} = 3.986 \times 10^{14}$ m³/s².

```quiz
? Add $\vec{A} = (6, 8)$ and $\vec{B} = (-2, 3)$. What is the magnitude of the sum?
= 11.7 | 0.005 |
> $\vec{A} + \vec{B} = (4, 11)$, with magnitude $\sqrt{16 + 121} = 11.7$.
```

```quiz
? A car traveling 35 m/s brakes as hard as possible on a road with $\mu = 0.70$. What is its stopping distance, in m?
= 89.29 | 0.005 | m
> Deceleration $\mu g = 6.86$ m/s²; $d = 35^2/(2 \times 6.86) = 89.3$ m.
```

```quiz
? A ball dropped from rest falls 3.0 m. How fast is it going, in m/s?
= 7.668 | 0.005 | m/s
> $v = \sqrt{2gh} = \sqrt{58.8} = 7.67$ m/s.
```

```quiz
? A projectile is launched at 40 m/s, 30° above level ground. What is its range, in m?
= 141.4 | 0.005 | m
> $R = \dfrac{40^2 \sin 60°}{9.8} = 141$ m.
```

```quiz
? A 5400 rpm hard drive spins at what angular velocity, in rad/s?
= 565.5 | 0.003 | rad/s
> $5400 \times 2\pi/60 = 565$ rad/s.
```

```quiz
? A 60 kg person rides an elevator accelerating downward at 3.0 m/s². What does a scale under them read, in N?
= 408 | 0.002 | N
> $N = m(g - a) = 60 \times 6.8 = 408$ N.
```

```quiz
? A cart rolls down a frictionless 15° ramp. What is its acceleration, in m/s²?
= 2.536 | 0.005 | m/s²
> $a = g\sin 15° = 2.54$ m/s².
```

```quiz
? What is the speed of a satellite in a circular orbit of radius $6.9 \times 10^6$ m, in m/s?
= 7601 | 0.005 | m/s
> $v = \sqrt{GM/r} = \sqrt{3.986 \times 10^{14}/6.9 \times 10^6} = 7600$ m/s.
```

```quiz
? An object moves in a circle at constant speed. Which statement is correct?
+ It is accelerating toward the center, so a net inward force acts on it.
- Its acceleration is zero because its speed is constant.
- A centrifugal force pushes it outward and balances everything.
- Its acceleration points along its velocity.
> The direction of velocity changes, which requires centripetal acceleration and a net inward force.
```

```quiz
? What is the kinetic energy of a 1500 kg car at 20 m/s, in kJ?
```

```quiz
? A roller coaster starts from rest 25 m above the lowest point of the track. Ignoring friction, how fast is it at the lowest point, in m/s?
= 22.14 | 0.005 | m/s
> $v = \sqrt{2 \times 9.8 \times 25} = 22.1$ m/s.
```

```quiz
? An elevator motor lifts 800 kg up 15 m in 12 s. What power does it deliver to the load, in kW?
= 9.8 | 0.002 | kW
> $P = mgh/t = 800 \times 9.8 \times 15/12 = 9800$ W.
```

```quiz
? A 10 g bullet at 500 m/s embeds itself in a 2.0 kg block resting on ice. How fast does the block move afterward, in m/s?
= 2.488 | 0.005 | m/s
> $0.010(500) = 2.010\,v'$, so $v' = 2.49$ m/s.
```

```quiz
? Why do airbags reduce injuries?
+ They extend the stopping time, so the force needed for the same change in momentum is smaller.
- They reduce the occupant's momentum before the crash.
- They make the collision elastic.
- They increase the occupant's mass.
> $F\Delta t = \Delta p$; a longer $\Delta t$ means a smaller force.
```

```quiz
? An EV motor delivers 250 N·m at 6000 rpm. What is its power output, in kW?

```

```quiz
? What is the moment of inertia of a 4.0 kg solid disk of radius 0.25 m about its axis, in kg·m²?
= 0.125 | 0.002 | kg·m²
> $\tfrac{1}{2}MR^2 = \tfrac{1}{2}(4.0)(0.0625) = 0.125$ kg·m².
```

```quiz
? A 1.5 kg mass should oscillate at 1.2 Hz on a spring. What spring constant is needed, in N/m?
= 85.27 | 0.005 | N/m
> $k = m(2\pi f)^2 = 1.5 \times (7.54)^2 = 85.3$ N/m.
```

```quiz
? What is the resonant frequency of an LC circuit with $L = 1.0$ µH and $C = 100$ pF, in MHz?
= 15.92 | 0.005 | MHz
> $f = \dfrac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{(10^{-6})(10^{-10})}} = 15.9$ MHz.
```

```quiz
? In the mechanical-electrical analogy, a series RLC circuit's resistance corresponds to:
+ The damping coefficient (friction)
- The mass
- The spring constant
- The amplitude
> Resistance and damping both dissipate energy as heat and multiply the rate of change ($i$ or $v$).
```

```quiz
? A DC motor has a stall torque of 4.0 N·m and a no-load speed of 200 rad/s, with torque falling linearly in between. What is its maximum output power, in W?
= 200 | 0.002 | W
> Maximum power is at half speed: $\omega = 100$ rad/s, $\tau = 2.0$ N·m, so $P = 200$ W.
```
