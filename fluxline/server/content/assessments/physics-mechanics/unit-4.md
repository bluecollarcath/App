Covers Lessons 4.1–4.4: rotational kinematics, torque, equilibrium and moment of inertia; $\tau = I\alpha$, rotational energy, $P = \tau\omega$ and angular momentum; simple harmonic motion, pendulums and the LC analogy; damping, resonance, $Q$ and RLC circuits. Use $g = 9.8$ m/s².

```quiz
? A wheel spins up from rest to 600 rpm in 4.0 s. How many revolutions does it make during that time?
= 20 | 0.001 | rev
> Average speed is 300 rpm = 5 rev/s, for 4.0 s: 20 revolutions.
```

```quiz
? A 50 N force is applied 0.30 m from a bolt, at 70° to the wrench handle. What torque does it produce, in N·m?
= 14.1 | 0.005 | N·m
> $\tau = 0.30 \times 50 \times \sin 70° = 14.1$ N·m.
```

```quiz
? A 25 kg child sits 1.6 m from a seesaw's pivot. Where must a 20 kg child sit to balance, in m from the pivot?
= 2 | 0.001 | m
> $25(1.6) = 20d$, so $d = 2.0$ m.
```

```quiz
? What is the moment of inertia of a 2.0 kg rod 1.5 m long, rotated about one end, in kg·m²?
= 1.5 | 0.002 | kg·m²
> $\tfrac{1}{3}ML^2 = \tfrac{1}{3}(2.0)(2.25) = 1.5$ kg·m².
```

```quiz
? A 1.2 N·m net torque acts on a rotor with $I = 0.050$ kg·m². What is its angular acceleration, in rad/s²?
= 24 | 0.001 | rad/s²
> $\alpha = 1.2/0.050 = 24$ rad/s².
```

```quiz
? A flywheel with $I = 0.50$ kg·m² spins at 300 rad/s. How much energy does it store, in kJ?
= 22.5 | 0.002 | kJ
> $\tfrac{1}{2}I\omega^2 = \tfrac{1}{2}(0.50)(90{,}000) = 22{,}500$ J.
```

```quiz
? A motor delivers 15 N·m at 1500 rpm. What is its output power, in W?
= 2356 | 0.005 | W
> $\omega = 157.1$ rad/s; $P = 15 \times 157.1 = 2356$ W.
```

```quiz
? A hoop rolls without slipping down from a height of 1.5 m. What is its speed at the bottom, in m/s?
= 3.834 | 0.005 | m/s
> For a hoop, $mgh = \tfrac{1}{2}mv^2 + \tfrac{1}{2}mv^2 = mv^2$, so $v = \sqrt{gh} = 3.83$ m/s.
```

```quiz
? A spinning diver reduces her moment of inertia from 2.4 to 0.80 kg·m². If she started at 1.5 rev/s, how fast does she spin now, in rev/s?
= 4.5 | 0.002 | rev/s
> $2.4 \times 1.5 = 0.80\,\omega$, so $\omega = 4.5$ rev/s.
```

```quiz
? A 0.20 kg mass on a spring with $k = 80$ N/m oscillates with amplitude 0.10 m. What is its maximum speed, in m/s?
= 2 | 0.001 | m/s
> $\omega = \sqrt{80/0.20} = 20$ rad/s; $v_{\max} = A\omega = 2.0$ m/s.
```

```quiz
? What is the period of a 2.0 m pendulum, in s?
= 2.838 | 0.005 | s
> $T = 2\pi\sqrt{2.0/9.8} = 2.84$ s.
```

```quiz
? What is the resonant frequency of an LC circuit with $L = 2.0$ mH and $C = 50$ nF, in Hz?
= 15915 | 0.005 | Hz
> $f = \dfrac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{(2 \times 10^{-3})(50 \times 10^{-9})}} = 15.9$ kHz.
```

```quiz
? Add a 20 Ω resistor in series with that LC circuit. What is the circuit's $Q$?
= 10 | 0.002 |
> $Q = \dfrac{1}{R}\sqrt{\dfrac{L}{C}} = \dfrac{1}{20}\sqrt{\dfrac{2 \times 10^{-3}}{50 \times 10^{-9}}} = \dfrac{200}{20} = 10$.
```

```quiz
? An oscillator with $m = 0.50$ kg and damping $b = 0.20$ kg/s. Its amplitude decays as $e^{-t/\tau}$ with $\tau = 2m/b$. What fraction of the amplitude remains after 10 s?
= 0.1353 | 0.01 |
> $\tau = 5.0$ s, so $e^{-10/5} = e^{-2} = 0.135$.
```

```quiz
? A driven oscillator has very light damping (high $Q$). What does its response look like near the natural frequency?
+ A tall, narrow resonance peak
- A low, broad hump
- No peak at all
- A dip to zero
> High $Q$ means little damping: a peak about $Q$ times the static response, with a narrow bandwidth $\omega_0/Q$.
```
