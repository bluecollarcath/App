Covers Lessons 3.1–3.3: work, kinetic energy and the work-energy theorem; gravitational and spring potential energy, conservation of energy, power and efficiency; momentum, impulse and collisions. Use $g = 9.8$ m/s².

```quiz
? A 60 N pull at 25° above horizontal drags a sled 12 m along level snow. How much work does the pull do, in J?
= 652.5 | 0.005 | J
> $W = 60 \times 12 \times \cos 25° = 653$ J.
```

```quiz
? How much work does it take to lift a 15 kg box 2.4 m at constant speed, in J?
= 352.8 | 0.002 | J
> $W = mgh = 15 \times 9.8 \times 2.4 = 353$ J.
```

```quiz
? How much work does it take to compress a spring ($k = 500$ N/m) by 8.0 cm, in J?
= 1.6 | 0.002 | J
> $\tfrac{1}{2}kx^2 = \tfrac{1}{2}(500)(0.08)^2 = 1.6$ J.
```

```quiz
? A 4.0 kg cart speeds up from 3.0 m/s to 7.0 m/s. How much net work was done on it, in J?
= 80 | 0.001 | J
> $\Delta KE = \tfrac{1}{2}(4.0)(49 - 9) = 80$ J.
```

```quiz
? A puck slides at 8.0 m/s onto a rough surface with $\mu_k = 0.20$. How far does it slide, in m?
= 16.33 | 0.005 | m
> $d = \dfrac{v^2}{2\mu_k g} = \dfrac{64}{3.92} = 16.3$ m.
```

```quiz
? A pendulum is released from 0.45 m above its lowest point. How fast is it at the bottom, in m/s?
= 2.97 | 0.005 | m/s
> $v = \sqrt{2gh} = \sqrt{2 \times 9.8 \times 0.45} = 2.97$ m/s.
```

```quiz
? A roller coaster moves at 5.0 m/s at the top of a 30 m hill. Ignoring friction, how fast is it at a point 12 m high, in m/s?
= 19.44 | 0.005 | m/s
> $v^2 = 5.0^2 + 2(9.8)(18) = 377.8$, so $v = 19.4$ m/s.
```

```quiz
? A spring ($k = 300$ N/m) compressed 0.15 m launches a 0.50 kg block across a frictionless floor. How fast does the block leave, in m/s?
= 3.674 | 0.005 | m/s
> $\tfrac{1}{2}kx^2 = \tfrac{1}{2}mv^2$, so $v = x\sqrt{k/m} = 0.15\sqrt{600} = 3.67$ m/s.
```

```quiz
? A pump lifts 500 kg of water 12 m in 60 s. What useful power does it deliver, in W?
= 980 | 0.002 | W
> $P = mgh/t = 500 \times 9.8 \times 12/60 = 980$ W.
```

```quiz
? A motor draws 1.2 kW and delivers 900 W of mechanical power. What is its efficiency, in percent?
= 75 | 0.002 | %
> $900/1200 = 75\%$.
```

```quiz
? A 50 g golf ball leaves the club at 70 m/s after 0.50 ms of contact. What is the average force on it, in N?
= 7000 | 0.002 | N
> $F = \Delta p/\Delta t = (0.050 \times 70)/(0.50 \times 10^{-3}) = 7000$ N.
```

```quiz
? A 2000 kg truck at 15 m/s hits a stopped 1000 kg car, and they lock together. What is their speed just after, in m/s?
= 10 | 0.001 | m/s
> $2000(15) = 3000v'$, so $v' = 10$ m/s.
```

```quiz
? A 1.0 kg cart at 6.0 m/s hits a stationary 3.0 kg cart in a perfectly elastic collision. What is the 1.0 kg cart's velocity afterward, in m/s (forward positive)?
= -3 | 0.001 | m/s
> $v_1' = \dfrac{1 - 3}{1 + 3}(6.0) = -3.0$ m/s: it bounces back.
```

```quiz
? A 3.0 kg rifle fires a 20 g bullet at 300 m/s. What is the rifle's recoil speed, in m/s?
= 2 | 0.001 | m/s
> $0 = 0.020(300) + 3.0v$, so $v = -2.0$ m/s: 2.0 m/s backward.
```

```quiz
? In which situation is kinetic energy conserved?
+ A perfectly elastic collision
- A perfectly inelastic collision
- A car crash in which the cars crumple
- A ball of clay hitting the floor
> Momentum is conserved in all collisions without external forces; kinetic energy only in elastic ones.
```
