Covers Lessons 1.1–1.4: SI units and vectors, velocity and acceleration, constant-acceleration motion and free fall, projectiles and relative velocity, and circular motion. Use $g = 9.8$ m/s².

```quiz
? Convert 90 km/h to m/s.
= 25 | 0.001 | m/s
> $90/3.6 = 25$ m/s.
```

```quiz
? A 50 N force points 37° above the $+x$ axis. What is its $x$ component, in N?
= 39.93 | 0.005 | N
> $50\cos 37° = 39.9$ N.
```

```quiz
? A vector has components $(-3, -4)$. What angle does it make with the $+x$ axis, measured counterclockwise from 0° to 360°?
= 233.13 | 0.003 | °
> $\tan^{-1}(4/3) = 53.13°$, and the vector is in the third quadrant: $180° + 53.13° = 233.13°$.
```

```quiz
? A car accelerates uniformly from rest to 30 m/s in 10 s. How far does it travel, in m?
= 150 | 0.001 | m
> $a = 3$ m/s², and $\Delta x = \tfrac{1}{2}(3)(10)^2 = 150$ m.
```

```quiz
? A car traveling 20 m/s stops in 25 m. What was its deceleration, in m/s²?
= 8 | 0.001 | m/s²
> $a = \dfrac{v_0^2}{2\Delta x} = \dfrac{400}{50} = 8.0$ m/s².
```

```quiz
? An object's position is $x(t) = 2t^3$ m. What is its velocity at $t = 3$ s, in m/s?
= 54 | 0.001 | m/s
> $v = 6t^2 = 54$ m/s at $t = 3$ s.
```

```quiz
? A rock is dropped from rest from an 80 m bridge. How fast is it moving when it hits the water, in m/s?
= 39.6 | 0.005 | m/s
> $v = \sqrt{2gh} = \sqrt{2 \times 9.8 \times 80} = 39.6$ m/s.
```

```quiz
? A ball is thrown straight up at 20 m/s. How long until it returns to the thrower's hand, in s?
= 4.082 | 0.005 | s
> Up and down take equal times: $2v_0/g = 40/9.8 = 4.08$ s.
```

```quiz
? A ball is kicked at 30 m/s, 40° above level ground. What is its range, in m?
= 90.44 | 0.005 | m
> $R = \dfrac{30^2 \sin 80°}{9.8} = 90.4$ m.
```

```quiz
? A package slides horizontally off a 5.0 m high loading dock at 8.0 m/s. How far from the dock does it land, in m?
= 8.081 | 0.005 | m
> Fall time $\sqrt{2(5.0)/9.8} = 1.01$ s; horizontal distance $8.0 \times 1.01 = 8.08$ m.
```

```quiz
? A swimmer crosses a river at 5.0 m/s relative to the water, aiming straight across, while the current flows at 2.0 m/s. What is her speed relative to the shore, in m/s?
= 5.385 | 0.005 | m/s
> $\sqrt{5.0^2 + 2.0^2} = 5.39$ m/s.
```

```quiz
? A motor shaft spins at 1800 rpm. A point on a pulley 0.15 m from the axis moves at what speed, in m/s?
= 28.27 | 0.005 | m/s
> $\omega = 1800 \times 2\pi/60 = 188.5$ rad/s; $v = 188.5 \times 0.15 = 28.3$ m/s.
```

```quiz
? A car rounds a 30 m radius curve at 15 m/s. What is its centripetal acceleration, in m/s²?
= 7.5 | 0.002 | m/s²
> $a_c = 15^2/30 = 7.5$ m/s².
```

```quiz
? Which statement about a projectile at the top of its arc (no air resistance) is true?
+ Its vertical velocity is zero, but its horizontal velocity is unchanged.
- Its velocity is zero.
- Its acceleration is zero.
- Its horizontal velocity is zero.
> At the top only the vertical part of the velocity is zero; the horizontal part stays constant, and gravity still acts.
```
