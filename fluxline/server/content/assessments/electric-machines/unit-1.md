Covers Lessons 1.1–1.3: magnetic circuits, transformers, and electromechanical energy conversion. Use $\mu_0 = 4\pi\times10^{-7}$ H/m.

```quiz
? A 300-turn coil on a gapped core: the 1 mm gap has 4 cm² area and dominates the reluctance. What is the inductance, in mH?
= 45.24 | 0.005 | mH
> $\mu_0N^2A/\ell_g = 4\pi\times10^{-7}(90\,000)(4\times10^{-4})/10^{-3}$.
```

```quiz
? A 300-turn coil carries 0.5 A. What is the MMF, in A·turns?
= 150 | 0.001 | A·t
> $NI$.
```

```quiz
? A transformer steps 2400 V to 240 V. A 16 Ω load on the secondary looks like what on the primary, in Ω?
= 1600 | 0.001 | Ω
> $a^2Z = 100 \times 16$.
```

```quiz
? An open-circuit test gives 230 V and 90 W. What is $R_c$, in Ω?
= 587.8 | 0.005 | Ω
> $230^2/90$.
```

```quiz
? A transformer delivers 100 kW with 400 W core loss and 1.2 kW copper loss. What is its efficiency, in %?
= 98.43 | 0.002 | %
> $100/101.6$.
```

```quiz
? A lifting magnet has two 20 cm² pole faces at 0.8 T. What force does it exert, in N?
= 1018.6 | 0.005 | N
> $2 \times (0.8^2/2\mu_0)(20\times10^{-4})$.
```
