Covers Lessons 3.1–3.2: variable-speed drives and brushless permanent-magnet machines.

```quiz
? A fan runs at 60% speed. Using the affinity laws, what fraction of full power does it need?
= 0.216 | 0.001 |
> $0.6^3$.
```

```quiz
? A 230 V, 60 Hz motor on V/f control runs at 45 Hz. What voltage should it get, in V?
= 172.5 | 0.001 | V
> $230 \times 45/60$.
```

```quiz
? A motor has $K_t = 0.12$ N·m/A. What torque does it make at 10 A, in N·m?
= 1.2 | 0.001 | N·m
> $K_tI$.
```

```quiz
? A motor with $K_e = 0.04$ V·s/rad runs from 48 V. What is its ideal no-load speed, in rpm?
= 11459 | 0.002 | rpm
> $48/0.04 = 1200$ rad/s.
```

```quiz
? A PMSM with 5 pole pairs and $\lambda_m = 0.05$ Wb carries $i_q = 40$ A. What torque does it make, in N·m?
= 15 | 0.001 | N·m
> $1.5 \times 5 \times 0.05 \times 40$.
```

```quiz
? A 10-pole PMSM spins at 3000 rpm. What is its electrical frequency, in Hz?
= 250 | 0.001 | Hz
> 5 pole pairs × 50 rev/s.
```
