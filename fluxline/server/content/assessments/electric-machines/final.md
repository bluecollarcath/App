The final exam covers all three units of Electric Machines. Use $\mu_0 = 4\pi\times10^{-7}$ H/m. Numeric answers are accepted within about 1%.

```quiz
? A coil of 200 turns links a flux of 2 mWb. What is its flux linkage, in Wb·turns?
= 0.4 | 0.001 | Wb·t
> $N\Phi$.
```

```quiz
? A 4:1 transformer has 120 V on its secondary. What is the primary voltage, in V?
= 480 | 0.001 | V
> $aV_2$.
```

```quiz
? A short-circuit test gives 50 V, 20 A and 400 W. What is $R_{eq}$, in Ω?
= 1 | 0.001 | Ω
> $400/20^2$.
```

```quiz
? What is the magnetic pressure at 1.2 T, in kPa?
= 573 | 0.002 | kPa
> $1.2^2/(2\mu_0)$.
```

```quiz
? A solenoid's inductance changes by 5 mH per mm. What force does it make at 2 A, in N?
= 10 | 0.001 | N
> $\tfrac12(4)(5\ \text{H/m})$.
```

```quiz
? A 250 V DC motor has $R_a = 0.25\ \Omega$. What is its starting current at full voltage, in A?
= 1000 | 0.001 | A
> $250/0.25$.
```

```quiz
? A DC motor runs at 1800 rpm with 220 V back-EMF. What is $K\phi$, in V·s/rad?
= 1.167 | 0.003 | V·s/rad
> $220/(188.5)$.
```

```quiz
? A 2-pole, 50 Hz induction motor runs at 2910 rpm. What is its slip?
= 0.03 | 0.001 |
> $(3000 - 2910)/3000$.
```

```quiz
? An induction motor has 50 kW air-gap power at 2% slip. What is its mechanical power, in kW?
= 49 | 0.001 | kW
> $(1 - s)P_{ag}$.
```

```quiz
? How many poles does a 50 Hz generator turning at 300 rpm have?
= 20 | 0.001 |
> $120(50)/300$.
```

```quiz
? A generator with $E_f = 1.6$, $V = 1.0$, $X_s = 1.6$ pu has what maximum power, in pu?
= 1 | 0.001 | pu
> $E_fV/X_s$.
```

```quiz
? A pump slowed from full speed to 50% uses what fraction of full power?
= 0.125 | 0.001 |
> $0.5^3$.
```

```quiz
? A 400 V, 50 Hz motor on V/f control at 20 Hz should receive what voltage, in V?
= 160 | 0.001 | V
> $400 \times 20/50$.
```

```quiz
? What is the $K_v$ of a motor with $K_e = 0.005$ V·s/rad, in rpm/V?
= 1909.9 | 0.005 | rpm/V
> $60/(2\pi \times 0.005)$.
```
