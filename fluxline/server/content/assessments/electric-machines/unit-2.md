Covers Lessons 2.1–2.3: DC machines, induction machines, and synchronous machines.

```quiz
? A DC motor with $K\phi = 2.2$ V·s/rad draws 12 A. What torque does it produce, in N·m?
= 26.4 | 0.001 | N·m
> $K\phi I_a$.
```

```quiz
? A 220 V DC motor with $R_a = 0.6\ \Omega$ and $K\phi = 2.2$ draws 15 A. What is its speed, in rad/s?
= 95.91 | 0.002 | rad/s
> $(220 - 9)/2.2$.
```

```quiz
? What is the synchronous speed of an 8-pole, 60 Hz motor, in rpm?
= 900 | 0.001 | rpm
> $120(60)/8$.
```

```quiz
? That motor runs at 870 rpm. What is its slip?
= 0.03333 | 0.005 |
> $30/900$.
```

```quiz
? An induction motor has 30 kW of air-gap power at 4% slip. What is its rotor copper loss, in W?
= 1200 | 0.001 | W
> $sP_{ag}$.
```

```quiz
? A 6-pole, 60 Hz induction motor has 25 kW of air-gap power. What is its torque, in N·m?
= 198.9 | 0.005 | N·m
> $\omega_s = 1200$ rpm $= 125.7$ rad/s.
```

```quiz
? A generator has $E_f = 1.2$, $V = 1.1$, $X_s = 0.9$ pu and $\delta = 20°$. What real power does it deliver, in pu?
= 0.5016 | 0.005 | pu
> $(1.2)(1.1)\sin20°/0.9$.
```

```quiz
? An over-excited synchronous machine:
+ supplies reactive power to the grid, like a capacitor
- absorbs reactive power, like an inductor
- runs above synchronous speed
- delivers no real power
> Raising $E_f$ raises the reactive power supplied.
```
