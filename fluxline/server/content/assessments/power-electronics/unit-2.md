Covers Lessons 2.1–2.2: rectifiers, power quality, and inverters.

```quiz
? What is the average output of a single-phase diode bridge on 120 V RMS, in V?
= 108 | 0.002 | V
> $0.9 \times 120$.
```

```quiz
? A three-phase diode bridge runs from 208 V line-to-line. What is its DC output, in V?
= 280.8 | 0.003 | V
> $1.35 \times 208$.
```

```quiz
? A thyristor bridge on the same supply fires at $\alpha = 45°$. What is its average output, in V?
= 198.6 | 0.005 | V
> $280.8\cos45°$.
```

```quiz
? A load has 30% current THD and a 0.98 displacement factor. What is its power factor?
= 0.9387 | 0.005 |
> $0.98/\sqrt{1.09}$.
```

```quiz
? A full-bridge square-wave inverter runs from 48 V. What is the peak of its fundamental, in V?
= 61.12 | 0.005 | V
> $4(48)/\pi$.
```

```quiz
? A three-phase SPWM inverter runs from 700 V at $m_a = 1$. What is its line-to-line RMS output, in V?
= 428.7 | 0.005 | V
> $0.6124 \times 700$.
```
