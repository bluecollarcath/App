Covers Lessons 1.1–1.3: the CMOS inverter, static CMOS gates, and fabrication and layout.

```quiz
? A node with $\alpha = 0.2$ and 15 fF switches at 2 GHz from a 1.0 V supply. What is its dynamic power, in µW?
= 6 | 0.001 | µW
> $\alpha CV^2f = 0.2 \times 15\times10^{-15} \times 1 \times 2\times10^9$.
```

```quiz
? How many transistors does a static CMOS 4-input NOR gate use?
= 8 | 0.001 |
> One NMOS and one PMOS per input.
```

```quiz
? A NAND2 is sized NMOS 2, PMOS 2. What is its logical effort (relative to an inverter with input capacitance 3)?
= 1.3333 | 0.002 |
> Input capacitance 4 versus 3.
```

```quiz
? With $D_0 = 0.15$ cm⁻², what is the Poisson yield of a 1.5 cm² die?
= 0.7985 | 0.002 |
> $e^{-0.225}$.
```

```quiz
? A wafer gives 400 dies at 75% yield and costs \$9000. What is the cost per good die, in dollars?
= 30 | 0.001 |
> $9000/(400 \times 0.75)$.
```
