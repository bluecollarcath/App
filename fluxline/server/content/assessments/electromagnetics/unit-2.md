Covers Lessons 2.1–2.3: vector analysis, electrostatics with boundary conditions, and magnetostatics.

```quiz
? What is $\nabla\cdot\mathbf A$ at $(1, 1, 1)$ for $\mathbf A = x^2\hat{\mathbf x} + y^2\hat{\mathbf y} + z^2\hat{\mathbf z}$?
= 6 | 0.001 |
> $2x + 2y + 2z = 6$.
```

```quiz
? $V = 6x - 8y$ volts. What is the magnitude of $\mathbf E$, in V/m?
= 10 | 0.001 | V/m
> $|\nabla V| = \sqrt{36 + 64} = 10$.
```

```quiz
? What is the spherical radius $r$ of the point $(2, 3, 6)$?
= 7 | 0.001 |
> $\sqrt{4 + 9 + 36} = 7$.
```

```quiz
? A coax has $\ln(b/a) = 1$ and $\varepsilon_r = 2$. What is $C'$, in pF/m?
= 111.3 | 0.005 | pF/m
> $2\pi\varepsilon_0(2)/1 = 111$ pF/m.
```

```quiz
? At a charge-free interface, $E_{1n} = 10$ V/m in air. What is $E_{2n}$ in a dielectric with $\varepsilon_r = 5$, in V/m?
= 2 | 0.001 | V/m
> $D_n$ is continuous: $10 = 5E_{2n}$.
```

```quiz
? What is $B$ 2 cm from a long wire carrying 5 A, in µT?
= 50 | 0.001 | µT
> $\mu_0I/(2\pi\rho) = 2\times10^{-7}(5)/0.02 = 50\ \mu$T.
```

```quiz
? A long air-core solenoid has 2000 turns/m carrying 0.5 A. What is $B$ inside, in mT?
= 1.257 | 0.005 | mT
> $\mu_0nI = 4\pi\times10^{-7} \times 1000 = 1.26$ mT.
```

```quiz
? A coax with non-magnetic insulation has $\ln(b/a) = 1$. What is $L'$, in nH/m?
= 200 | 0.001 | nH/m
> $\mu_0/(2\pi) = 200$ nH/m.
```
