Covers Lessons 4.1–4.2: waveguides and antennas.

```quiz
? An air-filled rectangular guide has $a = 10$ cm. What is its TE$_{10}$ cutoff, in GHz?
= 1.5 | 0.001 | GHz
> $c/2a = 3\times10^8/0.2$.
```

```quiz
? A guide with $a = 2$ cm is filled with a dielectric of $\varepsilon_r = 2.25$. What is its TE$_{10}$ cutoff, in GHz?
= 5 | 0.001 | GHz
> $c/(2a\sqrt{\varepsilon_r}) = 7.5\ \text{GHz}/1.5$.
```

```quiz
? A mode operates at twice its cutoff frequency. What is $\lambda_g/\lambda$?
= 1.155 | 0.005 |
> $1/\sqrt{1 - 0.25} = 1.155$.
```

```quiz
? What is the radiation resistance of a Hertzian dipole $\lambda/10$ long, in Ω?
= 7.896 | 0.005 | Ω
> $80\pi^2/100 = 7.90\ \Omega$.
```

```quiz
? What is the free-space path loss at 2.4 GHz over 1 km, in dB?
= 100.05 | 0.002 | dB
> $20\log_{10}(4\pi \cdot 1000/0.125) = 100.0$ dB.
```

```quiz
? A 2.4 GHz link has 20 dBm transmit power and 6 dBi antennas at both ends, 1 km apart. What is the received power, in dBm?
= -68.05 | 0.005 | dBm
> $20 + 6 + 6 - 100.05 = -68.0$ dBm.
```

```quiz
? What is the directivity of a half-wave dipole, in dBi?
= 2.15 | 0.01 | dBi
> $10\log_{10}1.64 = 2.15$ dBi.
```

```quiz
? In a waveguide, signals and energy travel at:
+ the group velocity, which is less than $u$
- the phase velocity, which exceeds $u$
- exactly $c$ regardless of frequency
- zero velocity below cutoff and infinite above
> $u_g = u\sqrt{1 - (f_c/f)^2}$; the phase velocity carries no information.
```
