The final exam covers all four units of Engineering Electromagnetics. Use $\eta_0 = 377\ \Omega$ and $c = 3\times10^8$ m/s. Numeric answers are accepted within about 1%.

```quiz
? Signals travel at $2\times10^8$ m/s on a cable. What is the delay of a 10 m length, in ns?
= 50 | 0.001 | ns
> $10/(2\times10^8) = 50$ ns.
```

```quiz
? A 150 Ω load terminates a 50 Ω line. What is the VSWR?
= 3 | 0.001 |
> $\Gamma = 0.5$, so VSWR $= 1.5/0.5 = 3$.
```

```quiz
? A load has $|\Gamma| = 0.1$. What is its return loss, in dB?
= 20 | 0.001 | dB
> $-20\log_{10}0.1 = 20$ dB.
```

```quiz
? A 120 Ω load is seen through a quarter wavelength of 60 Ω line. What is $Z_{in}$, in Ω?
= 30 | 0.001 | Ω
> $Z_0^2/Z_L = 3600/120 = 30\ \Omega$.
```

```quiz
? $V_s = 10$ V with $R_s = 50\ \Omega$ drives a line terminated in 150 Ω. What is the final, steady-state load voltage, in V?
= 7.5 | 0.001 | V
> At DC: $10 \times 150/200 = 7.5$ V.
```

```quiz
? Moving $0.125\lambda$ toward the generator rotates a point on the Smith chart by how many degrees?
= 90 | 0.001 | °
> A full turn ($360°$) is $\lambda/2$, so $\lambda/8$ is $90°$.
```

```quiz
? What is the outward flux of the position vector $\mathbf r$ through a sphere of radius 1 m?
= 12.566 | 0.002 |
> $\nabla\cdot\mathbf r = 3$; $3 \cdot \frac43\pi = 4\pi$.
```

```quiz
? A coax with $b/a = 3.5$ has a polyethylene dielectric ($\varepsilon_r = 2.25$). What is $C'$, in pF/m?
= 99.92 | 0.005 | pF/m
> $2\pi\varepsilon/\ln3.5 = 99.9$ pF/m.
```

```quiz
? A 2 nC charge sits 2 cm above a grounded conducting plane. What force attracts it to the plane, in µN?
= 22.47 | 0.005 | µN
> $Q^2/(4\pi\varepsilon_0(2h)^2) = 4\times10^{-18} \times 8.99\times10^9/0.0016 = 22.5\ \mu$N.
```

```quiz
? A core with $\mu_r = 2000$, $A = 0.5\ \text{cm}^2$ and $\ell = 5$ cm carries 50 turns. What is its inductance, in mH?
= 6.283 | 0.005 | mH
> $\mu N^2A/\ell = 2000(4\pi\times10^{-7})(2500)(0.5\times10^{-4})/0.05 = 6.28$ mH.
```

```quiz
? What is the skin depth of aluminum ($\sigma = 3.5\times10^7$ S/m) at 1 MHz, in µm?
= 85.07 | 0.005 | µm
> $1/\sqrt{\pi \cdot 10^6 \cdot 4\pi\times10^{-7} \cdot 3.5\times10^7} = 85\ \mu$m.
```

```quiz
? A plane wave in free space has a 1 V/m peak electric field. What is its average power density, in mW/m²?
= 1.326 | 0.005 | mW/m²
> $E_0^2/(2\eta_0) = 1/754$ W/m².
```

```quiz
? Light in air enters water ($n = 1.33$) at $60°$ from the normal. What is the refraction angle, in degrees?
= 40.63 | 0.005 | °
> $\sin^{-1}(\sin60°/1.33) = 40.6°$.
```

```quiz
? An air-filled WR-90 guide has $a = 22.86$ mm. What is its TE$_{10}$ cutoff, in GHz?
= 6.562 | 0.005 | GHz
> $c/2a = 6.56$ GHz.
```

```quiz
? How many dB of extra free-space path loss does tripling the link distance add?
= 9.542 | 0.005 | dB
> Path loss grows as $20\log_{10}R$: $20\log_{10}3 = 9.54$ dB.
```
