Covers Lessons 1.1–1.4: transmission-line fundamentals, reflection and input impedance, the Smith chart, and transients.

```quiz
? A lossless line has $L' = 360$ nH/m and $C' = 100$ pF/m. What is $Z_0$, in Ω?
= 60 | 0.001 | Ω
> $\sqrt{360\times10^{-9}/100\times10^{-12}} = \sqrt{3600}$.
```

```quiz
? A 25 Ω load terminates a 50 Ω line. What is $\Gamma_L$?
= -0.3333 | 0.002 |
> $(25 - 50)/(25 + 50) = -1/3$.
```

```quiz
? A line has $|\Gamma| = 0.5$. What is the VSWR?
= 3 | 0.001 |
> $1.5/0.5 = 3$.
```

```quiz
? What impedance quarter-wave section matches a 12.5 Ω load to a 50 Ω line, in Ω?
= 25 | 0.001 | Ω
> $\sqrt{50 \times 12.5} = 25\ \Omega$.
```

```quiz
? An 80 Ω load is seen through exactly half a wavelength of 50 Ω line. What is $Z_{in}$, in Ω?
= 80 | 0.001 | Ω
> A half-wave line repeats the load impedance.
```

```quiz
? A shorted 50 Ω stub is $\lambda/8$ long. What is the magnitude of its (inductive) reactance, in Ω?
= 50 | 0.001 | Ω
> $Z_0\tan45° = 50\ \Omega$.
```

```quiz
? A 6 V step with $R_s = 50\ \Omega$ drives a 50 Ω line. What is the amplitude of the first wave launched, in V?
= 3 | 0.001 | V
> $6 \times 50/100$.
```

```quiz
? A TDR reflection returns 10 ns after launch on a cable with $u_p = 2\times10^8$ m/s. How far away is the fault, in m?
= 1 | 0.001 | m
> $u_pt/2 = 2\times10^8 \times 10^{-8}/2 = 1$ m.
```
