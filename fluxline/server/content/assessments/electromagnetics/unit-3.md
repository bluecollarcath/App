Covers Lessons 3.1–3.3: Maxwell's equations, uniform plane waves, and reflection and transmission at interfaces.

```quiz
? A 0.05 m² loop sits in a uniform field $B = 0.2\sin(100t)$ T normal to it. What is the peak EMF, in V?
= 1 | 0.001 | V
> $\omega BA = 100 \times 0.2 \times 0.05 = 1$ V.
```

```quiz
? A material has $\sigma = 0.01$ S/m and $\varepsilon_r = 4$. What is $\sigma/(\omega\varepsilon)$ at 1 MHz?
= 44.94 | 0.005 |
> $0.01/(2\pi \cdot 10^6 \cdot 4 \cdot 8.854\times10^{-12}) = 44.9$: it behaves as a conductor at that frequency.
```

```quiz
? What is the intrinsic impedance of a non-magnetic dielectric with $\varepsilon_r = 2.25$, in Ω?
= 251.2 | 0.005 | Ω
> $377/1.5 = 251\ \Omega$.
```

```quiz
? What is the wavelength of a 1 GHz wave in a non-magnetic medium with $\varepsilon_r = 9$, in cm?
= 10 | 0.001 | cm
> $c/(f\sqrt9) = 0.1$ m.
```

```quiz
? What is the skin depth of copper ($\sigma = 5.8\times10^7$ S/m) at 10 kHz, in mm?
= 0.661 | 0.005 | mm
> $1/\sqrt{\pi \cdot 10^4 \cdot 4\pi\times10^{-7} \cdot 5.8\times10^7} = 0.66$ mm.
```

```quiz
? A plane wave in air hits a non-magnetic dielectric with $\varepsilon_r = 2.25$ at normal incidence. What is $\Gamma$?
= -0.2 | 0.001 |
> $\eta_2/\eta_1 = 1/1.5$; $\Gamma = (2/3 - 1)/(2/3 + 1) = -0.2$.
```

```quiz
? What is the Brewster angle from air onto a medium with $\varepsilon_r = 2.25$, in degrees?
= 56.31 | 0.005 | °
> $\tan^{-1}1.5 = 56.3°$.
```

```quiz
? Light travels from a medium with $n = 2$ into air. What is the critical angle, in degrees?
= 30 | 0.001 | °
> $\sin^{-1}(1/2) = 30°$.
```
