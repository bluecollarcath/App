Covers Lessons 2.1–2.4: span and independence, rank and the four subspaces, orthogonality, and least squares.

```quiz
? What value of $k$ makes $(1, 2)$ and $(3, k)$ dependent?
= 6 | 0.001 |
> $(3, k)$ must be $3(1, 2)$.
```

```quiz
? A $4 \times 6$ matrix has rank 4. What is the dimension of its null space?
= 2 | 0.001 |
> $6 - 4$.
```

```quiz
? A connected circuit has 10 nodes and 15 branches. How many independent loops does it have?
= 6 | 0.001 |
> $15 - 10 + 1$.
```

```quiz
? What is the cosine of the angle between $(1, 0, 0)$ and $(1, 1, 1)$?
= 0.5774 | 0.002 |
> $1/\sqrt3$.
```

```quiz
? Project $(4, 2)$ onto $(1, 1)$. What is each component of the projection?
= 3 | 0.001 |
> $\frac{6}{2}(1, 1)$.
```

```quiz
? Fit $y = c_0 + c_1t$ to $(1, 2)$, $(2, 3)$, $(3, 5)$, $(4, 6)$. What is the slope $c_1$?
= 1.4 | 0.002 |
> Normal equations: $c_0 = 0.5$, $c_1 = 1.4$.
```
