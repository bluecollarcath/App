```quiz
? 3% of parts are faulty. A test flags 90% of faulty parts and 5% of good ones. Given that a part is flagged, what is the probability it is faulty?
= 0.3576 | 0.005 |
> $P(\text{flag}) = 0.027 + 0.0485 = 0.0755$; $0.027/0.0755 = 0.358$.
```

```quiz
? How many different 3-member teams can be chosen from 8 engineers?
= 56 | 0.001 |
> $\binom83 = 56$.
```

```quiz
? Six independent trials each succeed with probability 0.3. What is the probability of exactly 2 successes?
= 0.3241 | 0.002 |
> $\binom62(0.3)^2(0.7)^4 = 15 \times 0.09 \times 0.2401 = 0.324$.
```

```quiz
? Errors occur at an average of 3 per hour (Poisson). What is the probability of exactly 2 errors in an hour?
= 0.2240 | 0.002 |
> $e^{-3}3^2/2! = 0.224$.
```

```quiz
? A lifetime is exponential with rate 0.5 per year. What is the probability of failure within 3 years?
= 0.7769 | 0.002 |
> $1 - e^{-1.5} = 0.777$.
```

```quiz
? Gaussian noise has $\sigma = 0.2$ V. What is the probability it exceeds $+0.3$ V?
= 0.0668 | 0.005 |
> $Q(1.5) = 0.0668$.
```

```quiz
? $X$ is uniform on $[-1.5, 1.5]$. What is its variance?
= 0.75 | 0.001 |
> $3^2/12 = 0.75$.
```
